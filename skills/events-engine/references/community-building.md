# Reference: Community Building — East Bay CXO

The East Bay CXO is a community, not just an event series.
This reference covers how to grow, nurture, and activate the community
between dinners — not just run the events.

---

## Community Design Principles

**Who it's for:** CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, and VP-level technology leaders
at Bay Area enterprises. Invite-only. Quality over scale.

**What makes it valuable to members:**
- Honest peer conversations — not vendor pitches
- Access to others navigating the same decisions
- Early access to Teqfocus thinking and research
- Connection to the Salesforce/Snowflake ecosystem through Teqfocus's network

**Teqfocus's role:** Host and convener — not vendor.
The moment East Bay CXO feels like a sales vehicle, it loses value.

---

## Community Growth Framework

### Invitation Strategy
- Every dinner: each attendee can nominate 1 peer for the next dinner
- Teqfocus AEs: each AE can nominate 2 prospects/quarter — but only relevant, senior contacts
- Partner channel: Salesforce and Snowflake AEs can nominate their senior client contacts
- Quality filter: must be VP+ at a 500+ person Bay Area company

### Member Retention
- Personal follow-up from Andy within 48 hours of each dinner
- East Bay CXO Newsletter (2 sections: recap + upcoming) — see references/east-bay-cxo-newsletter.md
- Occasional 1:1 check-in from Avi between dinners for active members
- Exclusive content: early access to Teqfocus research, TeqTalk episodes

### Community Activation Between Events
- LinkedIn: Tag members (with permission) in relevant posts
- Warm intro requests: "I know someone at [company] dealing with [same challenge] — would you be open to a connection?"
- Content sharing: Share relevant articles directly, not via mass email
- Advisory: Ask members for input on Teqfocus research or new service ideas

---

## Dinner Theme Selection Framework

Themes that generate the best conversations:
1. **Tension-based:** "At what point does AI governance become a competitive disadvantage?"
2. **Decision-forcing:** "Should CIOs own AI strategy or cede it to the CDO?"
3. **Timely + specific:** "What Agentforce actually requires — a candid conversation"
4. **Peer validation:** "How are you measuring the ROI of your data investment?"

Avoid:
- Themes that are too broad ("Digital transformation")
- Themes that position Teqfocus as the answer before the conversation starts
- Anything that sounds like a conference panel topic

---

## Measuring Community Health

Track quarterly:
- Dinner attendance rate (returning vs new attendees)
- Newsletter open rate
- Referrals from members to new attendees
- Deals sourced or influenced from community contacts [VERIFY pipeline attribution]
- LinkedIn follows from members after dinners
