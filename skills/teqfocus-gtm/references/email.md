# Email Campaigns Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## Email Writing Principles

- **Never pitch in Email 1.** Open with insight, relevance, or a question — not a product.
- **Subject line = the only job is to get the open.** Curiosity or benefit. Under 50 characters.
- **Personalization signals effort.** Reference the industry, the role, or a relevant trigger event.
- **One CTA per email.** Not three options. One clear next step.

---

## Single Email Structure

| Section | Length | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject Line** | <50 chars | Curiosity or benefit-led. No "Check out our..." |
| **Preview Text** | <90 chars | Extends the subject — don't repeat it |
| **Opening Line** | 1 line | Industry-specific problem or observation. No "I hope this email finds you well." |
| **Body** | 3–4 short paragraphs | Problem → Insight → Teqfocus relevance → CTA |
| **CTA** | 1 line | Specific. "Book 20 mins," "Download the guide," "See the case study" |
| **Signature** | Standard | Name, title, Teqfocus, LinkedIn, phone |

**Total length:** 150–200 words for cold/warm outreach. 200–300 words for nurture or follow-up.

## Email Sequence Structure (3–5 Emails)

### Email 1 — The Insight Open
**Goal:** Get the open. Establish relevance. Do not pitch.
**Formula:** Industry observation + a question that makes them think
**CTA:** Soft — "Worth a read?" / "Curious if this maps to what you're seeing?"

### Email 2 — The Problem Frame
**Goal:** Name the specific problem they're likely experiencing. Make them feel understood.
**Formula:** "Most [persona] in [industry] we talk to are dealing with [specific problem]. Here's what we've found."
**CTA:** Link to a relevant resource (case study, blog, one-pager)

### Email 3 — The Proof Point
**Goal:** Introduce Teqfocus with evidence, not claims.
**Formula:** One client story. "[Industry type] client. Problem. What we did. The outcome."
**CTA:** "Want to see how we'd approach this for [their company/industry]?"

### Email 4 — The Differentiator
**Goal:** Address the "why not a different partner" question before they ask it.
**Formula:** What makes Teqfocus different — Summit credential + full-stack depth + industry IP. One specific proof.
**CTA:** "Book a 30-minute [specific session name]"

### Email 5 — The Break-Up (If No Response)
**Goal:** One last value add + honest close. No guilt. No pressure.
**Formula:** Share a relevant insight or resource. Close with: "If the timing isn't right, no problem — I'll check back in [timeframe]."
**CTA:** Resource link or calendar link — their choice

## Subject Line Formulas by Persona

### For CIOs / CTOs
- *"The integration problem most Salesforce builds leave behind"*
- *"Your AI mandate needs a data layer first"*
- *"When [industry] IT transformations stall — what's usually wrong"*

### For CDOs / Data Leaders
- *"Why 70% of AI POCs don't survive the data layer"*
- *"Snowflake + Salesforce: the architecture most teams get backwards"*
- *"The data governance conversation that's blocking your AI roadmap"*

### For RevOps / VP Sales
- *"Your Salesforce forecast is probably off. Here's why."*
- *"What a well-built CRM actually looks like at [company size]"*
- *"30 days to better pipeline visibility — no rebuild required"*

## Tone Rules for Email

- **Never:** "I wanted to reach out," "I hope this finds you well," "As a leader in the industry"
- **Always:** Open with an observation or a question — not about Teqfocus
- **Never:** Stack platform names in the first email
- **Always:** Name the industry and the role — generic emails get deleted
- **Never:** Multiple CTAs. One action per email.
- **Summit credential:** Surface in Email 3 or 4 — not Email 1. It's a trust signal, not an opener.
