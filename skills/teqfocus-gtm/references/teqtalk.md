# TeqTalk Podcast Repurposing Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## What Is TeqTalk
TeqTalk is Teqfocus' CXO-level podcast series featuring AI, Data, and Salesforce leaders across industries. Episodes cover real adoption stories, architecture decisions, and executive perspectives — not theory or vendor pitches.

**Positioning in GTM:** TeqTalk is proof of Teqfocus' ecosystem access and thought leadership credibility. Every repurposed piece should reinforce that Teqfocus sits at the intersection of enterprise AI, data, and Salesforce transformation — and that the people shaping those fields want to talk to us.

## Newsletter Format (TeqTalk Issue)

Follow this structure for every TeqTalk newsletter:

1. **Title** — Episode number + guest name + core topic (sharp, not generic)
   - ✅ *"TeqTalk #35: What a Public Healthcare CIO Learned About AI the Hard Way"*
   - ❌ *"TeqTalk Episode 35 — Healthcare and AI"*

2. **Episode Hook (2–3 lines)** — The sharpest or most counterintuitive thing the guest said. Not a summary — a pull that makes the reader want to listen.

3. **Guest Intro (3–4 lines)** — Who they are, what they've built or led, why their perspective matters. One credibility signal.

4. **Value Bullets (3–4 bullets)** — What listeners will take away. Outcome-framed, not topic-listed.
   - ✅ *"Why interoperability isn't a technical problem — it's a governance one"*
   - ❌ *"Discussion of healthcare data challenges"*

5. **Thematic Takeaways (2–3 sections)** — Each with:
   - A subhead (bold, sharp)
   - 2–3 bullet points expanding the theme
   - 1 pull quote from the guest (exact or close paraphrase)

6. **Use Case Spotlight (optional but preferred)** — If the episode covers a specific implementation story or industry use case, give it its own section. Challenge → Approach → Outcome format.

7. **Future Outlook (2–3 lines)** — Where does the guest think this is heading? What should the reader watch for?

8. **CTA with Episode Link**
   - *"Hear the full conversation: [Episode Title] → [link]"*

9. **Community Prompt** — One question to spark replies or engagement
   - *"What's the biggest data governance challenge you're navigating right now? Reply and let us know."*

**Total length:** 500–700 words
**Tone:** Crisp. Thought-leadership oriented. Practical and high-impact.

## LinkedIn Post (Insight Pull from Episode)

**Structure:**
- Hook = sharpest, most counterintuitive or provocative thing the guest said
- 3–4 lines expanding or reacting to the insight
- Attribution: *"— [Guest Name], [Title], TeqTalk Episode [#]"*
- CTA: *"Full episode → [link]"*

**Rule:** The post should stand alone as an insight — not as a promotion of the episode. The episode link is the bonus, not the point.

## Short-Form Reel Script (45–90 seconds)

**Structure:**
1. **Hook (0–5 sec):** Bold on-screen text + spoken claim. The thing that stops the scroll.
   - Must be the sharpest, most debatable, or most surprising thing from the episode
2. **Setup (5–20 sec):** Brief context. Who is saying this and why it matters.
3. **The Insight (20–60 sec):** The guest's key point — delivered directly, not summarized by a narrator
4. **The Land (60–80 sec):** One line that gives the viewer something to do with this insight
5. **CTA (80–90 sec):** *"Full episode on TeqTalk → link in bio"* or on-screen card

**Reel Writing Rules:**
- Write in spoken language — not written/formal
- One insight per reel — never try to summarize the whole episode
- The hook must work as on-screen text AND as an audio hook simultaneously

- `[DESIGN FLAG: guest name lower third]`

## YouTube Animation Brief

When creating a YouTube animation brief for a TeqTalk episode, include:

1. **Episode Context:** Guest, topic, key themes
2. **Animation Style:** Icon-based motion graphics, keyword highlights, minimal overlay (not full screen). Inspired by conversational podcast visual style (WTF Kamath-style overlays).
3. **Segment Breakdown:** Timestamp → on-screen visual description → keyword or stat to highlight
4. **Branding:** White `#FFFFFF` background. Headline and title text: `#59267E`. Accent bars and lower thirds: `#7751E2`. Logo top-left or bottom-right. All text on white base.
5. **Key Moments to Animate:** The 3–5 most quotable or data-heavy moments in the episode

## Repurposing Rules

- **Never summarize the whole episode** in a single post — extract one sharp insight
- **Guest attribution is always required** — never paraphrase without crediting
- **Episode link in every repurposed asset** — always give a path back to the full episode
- **Tone matches the guest's energy** — technical guest → more precise language; storytelling guest → narrative tone
- **Avoid:** "In this episode we discuss..." — too passive. Lead with the insight, not the format.
