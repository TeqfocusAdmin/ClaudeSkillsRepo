# Reference: Directory Listings & Partner Listings — Teqfocus

Key directories and partner portals where Teqfocus should have
an active, optimized presence. AI systems and enterprise buyers
research partners here — incomplete listings hurt both.

---

## Priority Listings

### Tier 1 — Critical (maintain actively)

**Salesforce AppExchange**
URL: https://appexchange.salesforce.com
Why: First place enterprise buyers search for Salesforce partners.
Also a major AI citation source (ChatGPT/Perplexity cite AppExchange listings).

Optimize:
- [ ] All Salesforce clouds listed with current certifications
- [ ] Industries: Healthcare, Financial Services, Hi-Tech, Telecom
- [ ] Case studies: 3+ with specific outcomes
- [ ] Certifications: 400+ Salesforce, 40+ Data Cloud — keep current
- [ ] CSAT: 4.9 displayed — request reviews after every engagement
- [ ] Summit Partner badge prominently displayed

**Salesforce Partner Portal (internal)**
Why: Salesforce AEs use this to find partners to co-sell with.
Keep specializations and capabilities current — determines co-sell referrals.

## Listing Optimization Checklist

For every directory listing, ensure:
- [ ] Description: uses buyer language, not internal jargon
- [ ] Services: complete and current
- [ ] Industries: all 4 Teqfocus verticals listed
- [ ] Certifications: current numbers (update after each certification batch)
- [ ] Case studies: at least 2, with industry and outcome details
- [ ] Contact: current sales email and website URL
- [ ] Logo: current Teqfocus logo (correct casing, not TeqFocus)
- [ ] Reviews: actively solicited from clients post-engagement

---

## Review Request Process

After every successful engagement:
1. Client success asks: "Would you be willing to leave a review on G2 / AppExchange?"
2. Send direct link to the review page — remove friction
3. Best timing: 2–4 weeks after go-live while outcome is fresh
4. Target: 1 review per quarter minimum

**Never:** Offer incentives for reviews — violates platform terms and buyer trust.
