# Salesforce AE Influence Reference — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## The Goal
Get Salesforce AEs to proactively mention Teqfocus to their accounts — before the customer asks. Not because they have to, but because they trust us to make their deal bigger and their customer happier.

**The AE's calculation:** Every partner they recommend reflects on them. Teqfocus earns the recommendation by making it easy, credible, and commercially beneficial for the AE.

---

## Salesforce AE Influence: What Works and What Doesn't

| Works | Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| Specific account type triggers ("bring us in when...") | Generic "we're a great partner" positioning |
| Short — AEs are busy, 3 bullets max | Long capability documents |
| Making it easy — "intro me in 2 lines" | Asking AEs to do complex co-sell prep |
| Outcome-led proof ("here's what happened for [industry type] customer") | Feature lists and certification counts |
| Industry-specific — the AE needs to match you to an account | Cross-industry, cross-persona genericism |
| Regular touchpoints — monthly check-in, not quarterly | Showing up only when you need a referral |
| Face time — Dreamforce, QBRs, partner dinners | Email-only relationships |

## Salesforce AE Relationship Cadence

| Activity | Frequency | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Intro email / first contact | One-time | Establish relationship |
| 1:1 coffee or call | Quarterly min | Stay top of mind, understand their book |
| Dreamforce partner lunch | Annual | Deepen relationship in person |
| Co-sell deal support | As needed | Earn trust through delivery |
| New case study share | Per new approved proof point | Give them something to talk about |
| Joint event invite | Per event | Include them in the community |
| "Thinking of you" check-in | Monthly | 1 relevant article, case study, or industry note |
