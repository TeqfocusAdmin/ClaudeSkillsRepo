## Section 2: The Partner Marketing Mindset

Partner marketing is fundamentally different from customer marketing. The audience is internal — partner AEs, partner managers, alliance teams. They have different motivations and a different definition of useful.

### What Partner AEs Actually Care About

**Salesforce AEs care about:**
- Closing their quota — Teqfocus must make their deals easier to win
- Customer success — a bad SI experience reflects on the AE who recommended them
- Trust — they only refer partners they have seen deliver
- Speed — they need an SI who moves when a customer is ready
- Coverage — which clouds and which industries does Teqfocus actually know

**Snowflake Partner Teams care about:**
- Platform adoption and consumption growth — more Snowflake in the architecture is their win
- Customer reference stories — proof the joint solution works in market
- Technical depth — can Teqfocus actually architect Snowflake correctly
- Deal registration and pipeline visibility — they want joint opportunity volume

### The Partner Marketing Golden Rule
**Make the partner look good to their customer.** Every asset, every pitch, every co-sell motion should make the Salesforce AE or Snowflake partner rep look smart for recommending Teqfocus. That is the only test that matters.

## Section 5: Partner Messaging Rules

### Always
- Write for the partner first — they are the primary audience, not the end customer
- Make it easy for an AE to explain Teqfocus in 30 seconds to their customer
- Lead with what Teqfocus does for the partner's deal — not Teqfocus' company story
- Use partner-native language: "co-sell," "deal registration," "territory," "quota," "ACV," "attach rate"
- Keep it short — partner assets are consumed in under 2 minutes
- End every asset with a specific action — referral process, meeting request, pipeline registration

### Never
- Write partner assets that read like customer pitch decks
- Use generic "partnership is important" language without specifics
- Make Teqfocus the hero — the partner's customer outcome is the hero
- Use: "leverage," "synergy," "holistic," "end-to-end solutions," "cutting-edge"
- Announce Summit Partnership as news in partner materials — they know the ecosystem

## Section 6: Partner Marketing Brief Intake Checklist

**Confirm before writing any partner asset:**

- [ ] **Partner** — Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks, or other?
- [ ] **Asset Type** — AE email, one-pager, MDF proposal, co-branded deck, event collateral, QBR?
- [ ] **Audience Within Partner** — AE / field sales, partner manager, alliance team, solutions engineer?
- [ ] **Industry Focus** — Vertical-specific or cross-industry?
- [ ] **Joint Scenario** — What customer situation is this built for?
- [ ] **Proof Point Available** — Joint customer win, approved case study, pipeline data?
- [ ] **MDF / Investment Ask** — Is there a specific budget request attached?
- [ ] **CTA / Next Step** — What do we want the partner to do after seeing this asset?

> Partner assets with no clear ask get filed and forgotten. Always end with a specific action.

## Section 9: MDF Justification Framework

When requesting Marketing Development Funds from Salesforce or Snowflake:

**MDF request structure:**
1. **Activity:** What specifically are we funding? (event, content, campaign)
2. **Target audience:** How many accounts, what tier, which verticals
3. **Expected outcomes:** Pipeline influenced, leads generated, brand impressions [VERIFY thresholds with partner]
4. **Teqfocus co-investment:** What are we contributing? (matching funds, time, content)
5. **Measurement plan:** How will we report results back to the partner?
6. **Timeline:** Start date, completion date, reporting date

**What Salesforce/Snowflake typically fund:**
- Joint events (CXO dinners with Salesforce/Snowflake presence)
- Co-branded thought leadership content
- Webinars featuring Salesforce/Snowflake speakers
- Industry-specific campaign materials

**What they typically don't fund:**
- Generic Teqfocus marketing
- Content that doesn't mention the partner
- Retrospective funding (after the activity)

## Section 10: Partner Newsletter Structure

The Salesforce or Snowflake-focused version of the GTM newsletter:

**Format:**
1. **Joint win or proof point** — a recent engagement where the partnership created value
2. **Co-sell signal** — what Teqfocus is seeing in the market that's relevant to the partner's AEs
3. **Upcoming opportunity** — next event or campaign where AEs can bring their accounts
4. **One resource** — a case study, white paper, or tool AEs can share with their accounts

**Length:** 200–350 words. AEs scan. Make every sentence earn its place.
**Send to:** Salesforce/Snowflake partner contacts, not end customers
**Frequency:** Monthly or after each significant joint activity
