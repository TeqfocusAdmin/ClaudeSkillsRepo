## The 4-Iteration Loop

This skill runs the full 4-iteration loop on each asset category. The loop runs across all 9 assets in sequence — not per asset individually (too slow). Jas sees one changelog per pass that covers all 9 assets.

### Pass 1 — Draft All 9
Complete first drafts of all 9 assets.

### Pass 2 — Benchmark Critique
Score each asset: "Is this better than what the best B2B tech podcast produces?" Newsletter: does it read like a curated intelligence brief or a summary? LinkedIn posts: does each one open on a hook that stops the scroll? Reel scripts: does the hook land in 3 seconds? Rewrite the bottom 30% of each.

### Pass 3 — CXO Audience Filter
For each asset: "Would a CDO save or share this?" "Would a CTO send this to their team?" "Does this make the target persona feel understood?" Cut anything generic. Sharpen anything vague.

### Pass 4 — Platform Optimisation + GTM Integration
Apply format constraints (LinkedIn 3000 char limit, reel <60 seconds, blog SEO structure). Flag the outbound email asset's target personas and industries for SDR sequence integration.

## Asset 1: TeqTalk Newsletter

**Format:** Insight-driven brief, not episode summary. 300–500 words. Written for a senior exec with 4 minutes.

```
SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS:
1. [Option — insight-led, not "Episode X"]
2. [Option — question format]
3. [Option — counterintuitive claim]

NEWSLETTER BODY:

[EPISODE HOOK — 1 sentence that earns the read]

GUEST: [Name], [Title], [Company]
[2-sentence intro — practitioner voice, no press release language]

THE 3 THINGS WORTH KNOWING FROM THIS EPISODE:
• [Insight 1 — specific, actionable]
• [Insight 2 — specific, actionable]
• [Insight 3 — specific, actionable]

THE MOMENT:
[The single most surprising or counterintuitive exchange — 2–3 sentences]
"[Pull quote]" — [Guest Name]

USE CASE SPOTLIGHT:
[One real-world application of the episode's central insight — 3–4 sentences]

WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOU:
[Direct address to the reader — what should they do differently based on this?]

[CTA — specific: Watch / Listen / Share]
[Episode link]

COMMUNITY PROMPT:
[One question that invites replies — drives engagement in next send]
```

## Asset 2–4: LinkedIn Posts ×3

**Rule:** Three genuinely different angles. Not 3 versions of the same post. Each targets a different insight from the episode and could stand alone.

**Format per post:** Hook line → 3–5 insight lines → 1 question → CTA + link. 800–1,300 characters.

```
POST 1 — [Angle: e.g., The Counterintuitive Take]
Target persona: [Primary]
[Full post — ready to copy/paste]

POST 2 — [Angle: e.g., The Data Point + Implication]
Target persona: [May differ from Post 1]
[Full post]

POST 3 — [Angle: e.g., The Human Story]
Target persona: [May differ]
[Full post]
```

## Asset 5: LinkedIn Article

**Format:** 800–1,200 words. Thought leadership. Built around the episode's central theme, not the episode itself. The episode is a source, not the subject.

```
HEADLINE: [Keyword-mapped, stakes-based — not "Recap of Episode X"]
SUBHEADLINE: [Supporting statement]

OPENING: [The provocation — 2 sentences that earn the scroll]
SECTION 1: [The problem — specific to the target persona]
SECTION 2: [The conventional wisdom and why it falls short]
SECTION 3: [The insight from this episode — guest as practitioner source]
SECTION 4: [The decision framework or actionable approach]
CLOSING: [The challenge to the reader + CTA]

KEYWORDS: [3–5 LinkedIn SEO terms integrated naturally]
```
