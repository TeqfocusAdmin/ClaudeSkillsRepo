## BANNED WORDS AND PHRASES

Never use these. Ever. In any output.

| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| leverage | use / apply / deploy |
| synergy | combined outcome / shared benefit |
| holistic | complete / across the full stack |
| best-in-class | [specific differentiator instead] |
| cutting-edge | [specific capability instead] |
| robust | [specific quality or quantity instead] |
| end-to-end solutions | [describe the scope specifically] |
| "excited to share" | [start with the insight or the prospect's situation] |
| "proud to announce" | [start with the outcome] |
| "unlock value" | [name the specific value] |
| "unleash potential" | [name the specific capability] |

Opening rules:
- Never start with "I" or "We"
- Never start an email with "Excited to share" or "Proud to announce"
- Open with: an observation, the prospect's situation, or the operational reality

## THE OFFER-STACK LADDER

### Visual

```
STEP 4 — Pilot-to-Production Factory (annual retainer)
    ↑ funded from compound value of Steps 2 + 3
STEP 3 — Industry Workflow Accelerator (6–10 weeks, one workflow)
    ↑ funded from Step 2 outcome
STEP 2 — Trusted-Context Blueprint (4–6 weeks, architecture + governance)
    ↑ funded from Step 1 savings identified
STEP 1 — Stack-Rationalization & Readiness Assessment (1–2 weeks)
    ← ALWAYS the commercial entry point on a first call
```

### Step 1 — Stack-Rationalization & Readiness Assessment

**What it is:** 30-minute discovery session + 1–2 week assessment
**What it produces:** 3 prioritized agent/data use cases the board can sign off on; current-state gap analysis; recommended accelerator selection
**Why it works:** Sized to sit inside CIO discretionary authority. No committee approval required. Gives the prospect a defensible roadmap before any major spend.
**Use it when:** First call, prospect has (or is about to have) Salesforce/Snowflake licenses, needs to justify budget for a broader program
**How to pitch it:**
"30-minute discovery, then a 1–2 week assessment. By the end you'll have three prioritized use cases the board can sign off on. Sized to sit inside your discretionary authority — no committee."

---

### Step 2 — Trusted-Context Blueprint

**What it is:** 4–6 week architecture + governance design engagement
**What it produces:** Zero-copy data contract design between Snowflake/data layer and Salesforce Data Cloud; AgentOps framework; industry-specific accelerator selection; HIPAA/compliance architecture where required
**Why it works:** Funded from the efficiency or cost savings identified in Step 1. Gives the prospect an architecture they trust before committing to build.
**Use it when:** Step 1 surfaced architecture gaps the prospect wants designed before committing to a pilot; prospect has a data governance mandate; prospect has had a prior failed AI POC
**How to pitch it:**
"4–6 weeks of architecture and governance design. By the end you'll have a zero-copy data contract between your data layer and Salesforce, an AgentOps framework, and the accelerator selection that matches your specific workflow."

---

### Step 3 — Industry Workflow Accelerator

**What it is:** 6–10 weeks to a production pilot on one workflow, one line of business
**Available accelerators:**
- Prior Authorization Acceleration (Healthcare Payers)
- Patient Engagement (Healthcare Providers)
- Underwriting Assist (Insurance)
- Claims Automation (Insurance)
- HCP Engagement (Life Sciences)
- Deal Intelligence & Renewal Assist (SaaS/Hi-Tech)

**What it produces:** Live workflow running in production with humans in the loop; outcome metric tracked from day 1; pilot report suitable for board or QBR presentation
**Why it works:** Funded from the business outcome identified in Step 2. The accelerator compresses build time — it is a pattern the team has shipped before, not a custom build from scratch.
**Use it when:** Prospect has bought in on architecture and is ready to ship something measurable to the business
**How to pitch it:**
"6–10 weeks to a production pilot on one workflow, one line of business. By the end you have [accelerator name] running, with humans in the loop, and an outcome metric the board sees in the next QBR."

---

### Step 4 — Pilot-to-Production Factory

**What it is:** Annual retainer covering multi-workflow expansion, ongoing AgentOps, AI champion network, shadow AI governance
**What it produces:** Continuous use case pipeline; quarterly workflow launches; AI governance program; Teqfocus as embedded AI operations partner
**Why it works:** Funded from the compound value of Steps 2 and 3. The prospect stops running one-off pilots and runs an ongoing AI factory with a single accountable partner.
**Use it when:** Prospect has shipped Step 3, boards have seen the outcome metric, the business now wants AI as an operating condition not a project
**How to pitch it:**
"Annual retainer. We run the workflow redesign, the AI champion network, the shadow AI governance, the continuous use case pipeline. You stop running pilots — you run a factory."

## LADDERING RULES

**First call always → Step 1. No exceptions.**

| Situation | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Prospect wants to skip to Step 3 | "Verify architecture is ready before committing. Flag to Chintan or Ameya before scoping — he'll judge whether to compress Step 2 into Step 3 or hold the line." |
| Prospect wants Step 4 from cold | "Step 4 is sold from compound value of 2 and 3. You can shape the conversation around the Factory as the destination, but the commercial entry is still Step 1. Selling Step 4 on first call gets the prospect spooked or starts a discount fight." |
| Prospect says budget is already approved for a big program | "That's great context — the Step 1 Assessment produces the roadmap that justifies spending the budget correctly. It's still the entry." |
| Prospect wants to DIY their Step 2 architecture | "Possible — if they have the internal capability. The question to ask: 'Has your team shipped the Snowflake zero-copy contract before? That's where most internal teams stall.' Let the answer drive whether they need Step 2." |
