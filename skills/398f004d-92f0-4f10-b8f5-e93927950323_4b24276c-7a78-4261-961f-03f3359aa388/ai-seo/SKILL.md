## The AI Citation Framework

### What AI Systems Cite

Based on the Princeton GEO research (KDD 2024) and platform analysis:

**Highest citation signals:**
1. **Statistics + Fluency** — specific verified numbers in readable prose
   → "Teqfocus holds 400+ Salesforce certifications including 40+ Data Cloud
   certifications" outperforms "Teqfocus is highly certified"
2. **Third-party mentions** — AI cites where you appear, not just your site

## Content Format Rules for AI Citability

**Structure every page AI should cite:**
- Concise answer in first paragraph — AI extracts opening summaries
- H2/H3 headers matching exact question phrasing ("What is..." "How does...")
- Q&A blocks: `**Q:** ... **A:** ...` format — AI extracts these directly
- Specific numbers, not vague claims
- Author attribution — named Teqfocus practitioner adds authority signal
- Last-updated date visible — AI prefers recent content

**Avoid:**
- Gating key information behind forms (AI won't cite it)
- JavaScript-rendered content (AI can't extract it)
- Keyword stuffing — reads as low-quality, gets skipped
- Writing for the algorithm — content must genuinely answer the question
