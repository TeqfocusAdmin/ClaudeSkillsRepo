# Teqfocus Core GTM Positioning Reference

This document provides the essential Teqfocus positioning that every LinkedIn post must align with.

## The Bridge-the-Gap Narrative (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

**Core positioning statement:**
Teqfocus bridges the gap between what technology vendors PROMISE and what end users ACTUALLY GET as business outcomes.

**Three pillars that support this:**
1. **Technology expertise** — Deep platform knowledge across Salesforce, Snowflake, AWS/Azure/GCP, Databricks, Tableau
2. **Industry expertise** — Healthcare, Telecom, Financial Services, Retail/CPG, Hi-Tech
3. **Decades of experience** — We've seen the patterns, the gaps, and the failure modes

**How to use it:**
Every post must explicitly or implicitly show:
- Vendor X promises Y outcome
- Enterprise buyers want Y outcome
- But there's a gap between promise and delivery
- Teqfocus closes that gap using [technology expertise + industry expertise + experience]

## Target Personas (Industry-Specific)

### CIO / CTO
- **Fear:** Another transformation that underdelivers, creates debt, leaves a mess
- **Entry Point:** Risk reduction + integration credibility + industry proof
- **Buying Trigger:** Failed project, AI board mandate, M&A integration, cloud migration stall

### CDO / Head of Data & AI
- **Fear:** AI that the business doesn't trust or use; data strategy without adoption
- **Entry Point:** Data maturity + AI readiness + governed pipelines
- **Buying Trigger:** Failed POC, Data Cloud mandate, CEO asking "why isn't AI working"

### Mid-Market RevOps / Ops Leader
- **Fear:** Salesforce build the sales team ignores; customization that breaks every release
- **Entry Point:** Speed + adoption + practical outcomes in 90 days
- **Buying Trigger:** Salesforce renewal, new VP of Sales, poor adoption, growth breaking process

### VP of Sales / Revenue Leader
- **Fear:** Salesforce as an expensive CRM nobody uses; no pipeline visibility
- **Entry Point:** CRM that actually drives revenue, not just tracks it
- **Buying Trigger:** Missed quarters, manual forecasting, new sales motions

## Industry Messaging Frameworks

### Healthcare
**Lead Pain:** Fragmented patient data, care gaps, SDOH integration, HIPAA complexity
**Teqfocus Angle:** AI-powered care coordination, interoperability, clinical data unification
**Primary Stack:** Health Cloud, Data Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks
**Bridge-the-Gap Example:** "Health Cloud promises a unified patient view. But between vendor demo and live deployment, data sits in Epic, claims in Facets, SDOH in community systems. We connect all three — so care teams see what they were promised."

### Financial Services
**Lead Pain:** Advisor productivity gaps, compliance risk, fragmented client 360
**Teqfocus Angle:** Compliant AI workflows, advisor intelligence, unified client data
**Primary Stack:** FinServ Cloud, Data Cloud, Snowflake, Tableau
**Bridge-the-Gap Example:** "Financial Services Cloud promises advisor productivity. But wealth management data lives across custody, trading, and CRM platforms. We unify it under governance — so advisors actually use what they were promised."

### Retail / CPG
**Lead Pain:** Disconnected commerce + service data, loyalty gaps, demand forecasting
**Teqfocus Angle:** Unified commerce, AI personalization, distributor portals
**Primary Stack:** Commerce Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau
**Bridge-the-Gap Example:** "Commerce Cloud promises personalized customer experiences. But e-commerce data, in-store transactions, and loyalty programs live in silos. We connect them — so marketing sees the unified view they were promised."

### Telecom
**Lead Pain:** Churn prediction gaps, field service inefficiency, network complexity, fragmented CX
**Teqfocus Angle:** Predictive churn models, field service AI, omnichannel CX
**Primary Stack:** Service Cloud, Field Service, Databricks, AWS
**Bridge-the-Gap Example:** "Service Cloud promises omnichannel support. But network data, billing systems, and CRM live apart. We integrate them — so agents see the complete picture they were promised."

### Hi-Tech / SaaS
**Lead Pain:** Slow sales cycles, CS churn risk, PLG ↔ CRM misalignment
**Teqfocus Angle:** CPQ optimization, renewal automation, CS-to-sales handoffs
**Primary Stack:** Sales Cloud, CPQ, Agentforce, Databricks
**Bridge-the-Gap Example:** "Sales Cloud promises pipeline visibility. But product usage data lives in your app, not your CRM. We connect both — so reps see the expansion signals they were promised."
