# SDR Call Scripts & Meeting Opener Reference — Teqfocus Sales Enablement

## Purpose
Scripts for SDR cold calls, warm follow-up calls, and meeting openers for Account Executives. Written to sound like a human conversation — not a script being read.

**Rule:** Sound prepared, not rehearsed. Know the words well enough that you don't need them.

---

## Cold Call Structure (90-Second Version)

**Goal:** Get 30 seconds of their attention → establish relevance → earn a meeting

```
[Opening — no pleasantry]
"[Name], this is [Your Name] from Teqfocus — 
I'll be quick, I know you didn't expect my call."

[Pattern interrupt — industry observation, not a pitch]
"I work with [persona type] leaders in [industry] — 
and the problem we're seeing most right now is [specific, 
named problem in 1 line]."

[The hook — why now]
"The reason I'm calling specifically is [relevant trigger: 
recent news about their company, common industry shift, 
or specific thing that makes this timely]."

[The ask — specific and low-friction]
"I don't want to pitch you on a call you didn't ask for —
but I think there's a 20-minute conversation worth having 
about [specific topic]. Would [day] or [day] work 
for something that focused?"
```

## Cold Call by Persona

### For CIO / CTO
```
"[Name], this is [Name] from Teqfocus — I'll be brief.

I work with enterprise IT leaders in [industry], and the 
pattern we keep seeing is AI mandates landing on the CTO's 
desk before the data infrastructure is ready to support them.

[If relevant trigger available: "I saw [company] recently 
announced [initiative] — that's usually the inflection point 
where the architecture question becomes urgent."]

Is that a conversation worth 20 minutes? I can make it 
specific to where your team is right now."
```

### For CDO / Head of Data & AI
```
"[Name], this is [Name] from Teqfocus.

Quick context: we work with data and AI leaders in [industry] 
on the gap between having a modern data platform and having 
AI that the business actually trusts and uses.

Most of the CDOs I talk to right now have the infrastructure 
in place but are stuck on the adoption or ROI question.

Is that something you're navigating — and would 20 minutes 
be worth it to compare notes?"
```

### For RevOps / VP Sales
```
"[Name], this is [Name] from Teqfocus.

We work with sales ops and RevOps leaders on Salesforce — 
specifically the gap between what the CRM is supposed to do 
and what the sales team actually does inside it.

The pattern we see a lot is adoption sitting at 40–50% 
with the same rep behaviors outside the system that existed 
before the implementation.

Is that familiar — and would a focused 20-minute conversation 
be useful?"
```

## Warm Follow-Up Call (After Email / LinkedIn Engagement)

**Use when:** Prospect opened emails, clicked a link, or engaged with LinkedIn content — but hasn't responded.

```
"[Name], this is [Name] from Teqfocus.

I sent you a note recently about [topic from email/post] — 
I'm not sure if it landed in the right place, but I wanted 
to follow up directly because [specific reason this is 
relevant to them — 1 line].

I'm not going to pitch you on this call — I just want to 
ask: is [specific problem] something that's on your radar 
right now, or is it not the right timing?

Either answer is useful for me."
```

## Voicemail Script (Under 30 Seconds)

```
"[Name], this is [Name] from Teqfocus — 
[phone number, said slowly].

I work with [persona] leaders in [industry] on 
[specific problem in 5 words]. I have something 
specific I wanted to run by you — not a pitch, 
just a 2-minute question.

[Phone number again]. Happy to connect whenever 
works for you."
```

**Rules for voicemail:**
- Under 30 seconds — always
- Say your number twice, slowly
- Name one specific thing — not "a range of solutions"
- Never say "I wanted to reach out" or "I hope you're doing well"
- The goal is a callback — not to explain everything

## AE Meeting Opener (First Discovery Call)

**Use for:** First formal meeting with a prospect — often booked by SDR.

```
[30-second opener — sets the agenda and puts them at ease]

"Thanks for making time, [Name]. 

Here's how I'd like to use our time: I'll spend about 
5 minutes giving you context on Teqfocus and why I thought 
this conversation made sense — and then I'd like to spend 
most of the time understanding where you are with [topic] 
and what's driving your thinking right now.

Fair?"

[If they say yes — go straight to Zone 1 discovery]
[If they want you to pitch first — do a 3-minute version and 
 get to questions as fast as possible]
```

## Call Rules

1. **Never read from a script** — internalize the structure, speak naturally
2. **Personalize the opening** — a generic opener signals you haven't done the work
3. **Get to the relevance signal fast** — why are you calling THIS person at THIS company NOW
4. **Ask for a specific meeting, not "some time"** — "Tuesday or Thursday" converts better than "sometime this week"
5. **Handle the gatekeep gracefully** — "Is [Name] the right person for a conversation about [topic], or would someone else be better?"
6. **Log every call** — even if no answer. Patterns matter.
7. **Don't pitch on a cold call** — your job is to earn 20 minutes, not close the deal
