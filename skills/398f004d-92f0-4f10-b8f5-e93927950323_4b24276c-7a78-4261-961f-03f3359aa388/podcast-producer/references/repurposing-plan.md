# Episode Repurposing Plan — TeqTalk Podcast Producer

## Purpose
The repurposing plan turns one episode recording into a full multi-channel content program. The plan defines what assets to build, in what order, and when each goes live — so nothing is left on the table and distribution is consistent.

**Core principle:** One episode should generate 2–3 weeks of content across email, LinkedIn, and YouTube. That's the compounding value of a well-produced podcast.

## Repurposing Decisions: Choosing the Right Moments

Not every moment deserves every asset type. Use this guide to match key moments to the right format.

| Moment Type | Best Asset Format |
|---|---|
| Episode's sharpest contrarian claim | Reel #1 hook, Newsletter hook, LinkedIn post #1 |
| A complete story with a clear arc | Reel #2, LinkedIn Use Case post |
| A specific stat or data point | Quote card, Reel animated overlay, Newsletter bullet |
| Guest's future prediction | Reel #3, LinkedIn Outlook post, Newsletter Section 7 |
| A vulnerable / honest admission | Quote card, LinkedIn Question post |
| A practical framework or model | LinkedIn Article subhead, Newsletter Takeaway section |
| An architectural decision explained | YouTube animation key moment, LinkedIn Article |

## Distribution Rules

1. **Spread posts across the week** — not all on the same day. LinkedIn algorithm rewards consistent posting.
2. **Never publish two heavy pieces on the same day** — e.g., newsletter + LinkedIn Article on the same day competes with itself
3. **Reels should drop after the episode has a few days of organic traction** — not before it's published
4. **Quote cards work on any day** — they're filler-proof content
5. **Tag the guest on every public LinkedIn post** — their network amplifies reach
6. **Episode link in every asset** — without exception. The episode is always the destination.
7. **Week 3 content shouldn't feel stale** — write future-outlook and contrarian posts in a way that's evergreen, not time-stamped

## Guest Amplification Protocol

After the episode publishes, maximize the guest's own network:

**Day 0 — Episode publish:**
Email the guest: "Episode is live — here's the link. We've prepared some LinkedIn posts you can share if useful: [2 pre-written posts, tagged, ready to copy-paste]"

**Day 1–2:**
Tag the guest in TeqTalk's own LinkedIn post — their response/comment multiplies reach.

**Day 3–5:**
If the guest hasn't posted, send a friendly nudge: "The episode is getting great traction — here's a quick post you could share if you haven't already: [pre-written post]"

**Provide guest with:**
- Episode link
- 2–3 pre-written LinkedIn posts (they can choose or adapt)
- 1–2 quote card graphics (tagged and formatted for their profile)
- YouTube link (if applicable)

**Rule:** Make it as easy as possible for the guest to share. Every step they have to do themselves reduces the chance they do it at all.
