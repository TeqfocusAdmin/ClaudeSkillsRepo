# LinkedIn Content Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Asset Types Covered
- LinkedIn Post (organic feed)
- LinkedIn Article
- LinkedIn Carousel (document post)

---

## 1. LinkedIn Post (Organic Feed)

**Structure:**
1. **Hook (Line 1):** Bold claim, counterintuitive statement, or sharp question. Never start with "I" or "We." No emojis in the hook.
2. **Body (Lines 2–5):** 3–5 insight lines. One idea per line. White space between each.
3. **Bridge (Line 6):** Connect the insight to Teqfocus' POV or a relevant proof point — naturally, not as an announcement.
4. **CTA (Final line):** Specific. Drop the link, tag the guest, name the resource.

**Length:** 800–1,300 characters
**Tone:** Practitioner authority — not marketing copy. The smartest person in the room, not the loudest.

**Banned openers:** "Excited to share...", "Thrilled to announce...", "We are proud to...", "I", "We"

---

### Post Formats That Work for Teqfocus

**The Contrarian Take**
> "Everyone says [X]. Here's why that's wrong in enterprise AI."

**The Insight From the Field**
> "A CDO told us last week: [specific, real-sounding observation]. Here's what that actually means."

**The Framework**
> "3 reasons AI projects stall at the data layer — and what actually fixes them."

**The Industry Spotlight**
> "Healthcare CIOs aren't asking 'should we adopt AI?' anymore. They're asking [sharper question]."

**The Proof Point**
> "[Outcome achieved]. Here's the architecture that made it possible."

---

### Post Templates by Persona

**Targeting CDOs / Data Leaders**
- Hook: *"[Specific AI failure mode] isn't a model problem. It's a data problem."*
- Body: Name the data layer gap → explain why it kills AI adoption → frame Teqfocus' approach

**Targeting CIOs / CTOs**
- Hook: *"The most expensive Salesforce mistake isn't a bad license deal."*
- Body: Name the hidden cost (bad architecture, low adoption, integration debt) → what good looks like

**Targeting RevOps / VP Sales**
- Hook: *"Your Salesforce forecast is probably wrong. Here's why."*
- Body: Common CRM failure modes → what a well-built Salesforce actually delivers → Teqfocus proof point

## 2. LinkedIn Article

**Structure:**
1. **Thumbnail Title** — SEO-friendly, outcome-led, 8–12 words
2. **Challenge Intro** (2–3 paragraphs) — Open with the industry problem. Make the reader feel seen before offering anything.
3. **Core Value Section** (3–5 subheads, 2–3 paragraphs each) — One insight or use case per subhead. Connect to Teqfocus' stack where natural — not forced.
4. **Hook to Full Resource** — Link to related blog, case study, podcast episode, or webinar
5. **Caption + Hashtags** — 3–5 relevant hashtags. No more.

**Length:** 600–900 words
**Tone:** Thought leadership — bold POV backed by experience or data

## 3. LinkedIn Carousel (Document Post)

**Slide structure:**
- **Slide 1:** Bold claim or sharp question. Big type. `[DESIGN FLAG: White #FFFFFF background, headline #59267E Poppins Bold, accent bar #BD72EF at top or left edge]`
- **Slides 2–7:** One insight per slide. Short headline + 1–2 supporting lines.
- **Slide 8 (Last):** CTA + Teqfocus logo + website

**Content formats that work as carousels:**
- "5 Signs Your Salesforce Implementation Is Holding You Back"
- "The Data Layer Every AI Project Needs (And Most Companies Are Missing)"
- "How [Industry] Leaders Are Using Agentforce in 2025"
- "Why [X]% of AI Projects Fail Before They Go Live"

## TeqTalk Episode → LinkedIn

**Post format:**
- Hook = sharpest or most counterintuitive insight from the guest
- 3–4 lines expanding on the insight
- Attribution: *"— [Guest Name], [Title], TeqTalk Episode [#]"*
- CTA: Link to episode

**Article format:**
- Build a full thought leadership piece around the episode's central theme
- Guest quotes used as supporting evidence — not the main story
- CTA: *"Hear the full conversation → [link]"*

**Carousel format:**
- "5 Things [Guest Name] Said About [Topic] That Every [Persona] Should Hear"
- Each slide = one tightly paraphrased insight or quote
