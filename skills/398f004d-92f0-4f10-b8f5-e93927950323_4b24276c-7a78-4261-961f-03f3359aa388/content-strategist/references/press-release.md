# Reference: Press Release / Announcement

Length: 400–600 words | Format: inverted pyramid
Use for: Partnership announcements · Award recognitions · Major client wins ·
New service launches · Executive appointments

## Structure

### Dateline
`[CITY, Month Day, Year] —`

### Lead Paragraph (50–75 words)
The most important information first — who, what, when, where, why.
Must stand alone as a complete story.
No jargon, no adjectives — just the news.

**Template:**
"Teqfocus, a [descriptor], today announced [what happened].
[One sentence on why it matters / what it means for clients.]"

### Second Paragraph — Context (75–100 words)
- Why this matters in the market right now
- 1 supporting data point or industry context
- Sets up the quote

### Executive Quote (40–60 words)
Format:
```
"[Quote — insight or reaction, not a press release cliché],"
said [Full Name], [Title], Teqfocus.
"[Optional second sentence adding depth.]"
```
Rules:
- No "excited" / "proud" / "pleased to announce" — ever
- Quote should add information not in the body copy
- Reads like something a real person would say

### Detail Paragraph (75–100 words)
- Specifics: what exactly was announced
- Implementation details, timeline, scope
- Second quote from partner/client (if applicable)

### End marker: `###`

## Rules
- Inverted pyramid — most important news first
- No superlatives: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class"
- No passive voice
- American English throughout
- Flag any unverified stats with [VERIFY]
