# Co-Marketing Campaign Brief — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## What a Co-Marketing Campaign Is
A structured, time-bound program where Teqfocus and a partner (Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks) jointly fund, promote, and execute a marketing initiative — typically a webinar, event, content series, or demand generation campaign — targeting a shared customer audience.

**Co-marketing is most successful when:**
- Both parties have a clear and compatible audience to reach
- The topic is relevant to both platforms (not just one)
- Both parties commit to promoting — not just one side doing the work
- There's an agreed definition of a lead / MQL and how they're shared

## Co-Marketing Campaign Principles

1. **Agree on the audience before agreeing on the format.** The most common failure is building a webinar and then asking "who do we invite?" Confirm the audience first — then design the campaign around them.

2. **Both parties must promote.** A co-marketing campaign where only Teqfocus sends emails is just a Teqfocus campaign with a partner logo on it. Confirm the partner's promotion commitment in writing before investing in production.

3. **Lead sharing must be agreed before the campaign launches.** Discovering a lead sharing dispute after 300 registrations is avoidable — agree upfront.

4. **MDF is a business case, not a request.** See `mdf-justification.md` for how to structure the ask.

5. **One campaign should feed the next.** Capture insights, testimonials, and follow-up content from every campaign to build the next one.
