# Snowflake Partner Assets Reference — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## The Teqfocus + Snowflake Relationship

Teqfocus and Snowflake are natural co-sell partners. Most enterprise Snowflake deployments need help — architecture, implementation, governance, and connection to the applications the business runs on (Salesforce, custom apps). Teqfocus provides that layer. In return, Teqfocus implementations drive Snowflake consumption growth, which is what Snowflake field reps are measured on.

**The co-sell equation:**
- Teqfocus brings Snowflake into Salesforce-led deals where the customer needs a modern data foundation
- Snowflake brings Teqfocus into data platform deals where the customer needs Salesforce or application connectivity
- Both sides benefit from joint wins in Healthcare, FinServ, Telecom — Teqfocus' strongest verticals

## Snowflake Co-Sell Positioning

**For accounts evaluating Snowflake + Salesforce together:**
> *"Teqfocus is the SI that handles both sides. We architect the Snowflake foundation — data modeling, governance, pipeline — and connect it directly to Salesforce Data Cloud and the customer's AI layer. One partner, full accountability, no gap between the platforms."*

**For accounts already on Snowflake, evaluating Salesforce:**
> *"Teqfocus connects your existing Snowflake investment to Salesforce — so your CRM runs on governed, real-time data instead of a fragmented view. That protects your Snowflake investment and makes Salesforce actually perform."*

**For accounts with a failed or stalled Snowflake implementation:**
> *"Snowflake is straightforward to deploy. It's the architecture decisions underneath — data modeling, governance, what connects to it — that determine whether it delivers value. Teqfocus specializes in building Snowflake environments that grow with the business and connect to the application layer."*

## Snowflake Joint One-Pager: Key Messages

When building a Teqfocus + Snowflake joint one-pager, anchor on these messages:

**Headline options:**
- *"From Data Foundation to Business Intelligence: How Teqfocus and Snowflake Power Enterprise AI"*
- *"Why [Industry] Leaders Are Building on Snowflake + Salesforce — With Teqfocus"*
- *"The Architecture That Connects Your Data, Your AI, and Your CRM"*

**The 3 core messages:**
1. **Data-ready AI** — Snowflake provides the governed, scalable data foundation that AI initiatives require. Teqfocus builds the architecture and connects it to the intelligence and application layers.
2. **Salesforce + Snowflake — connected, not siloed** — Teqfocus is the partner that handles both. CRM data is enriched by Snowflake; Snowflake is activated through Salesforce workflows.
3. **Industry-specific depth** — In [Healthcare / Telecom / FinServ], the data model, compliance requirements, and workflows are specific. Teqfocus brings that vertical knowledge; Snowflake brings the platform scale.
