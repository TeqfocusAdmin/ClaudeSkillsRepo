# Partner Enablement Guide — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## Purpose
Partner enablement assets are internal documents that help Teqfocus team members navigate partner relationships effectively — and external documents that help partner AEs understand how and when to recommend Teqfocus.

---

## Internal Partner Enablement: Teqfocus Team Guide

### How to Work With Salesforce AEs

**The relationship dynamic:**
Salesforce AEs are quota-carrying reps. Every interaction should make their life easier or their deal bigger — or both. They don't have time for partner pitches. They have time for a 2-line "here's why Teqfocus fits this account" message.

**Conversation starters that work:**
- "Which accounts in your book have a Data Cloud or AI conversation alongside Salesforce?"
- "Where are you seeing implementation quality issues that are reflecting back on you?"
- "What deals are stalling because the customer doesn't have a data partner alongside Salesforce?"

**What to bring to the first meeting:**
- 1 industry-specific case study (relevant to their territory/vertical)
- 1 clear "when to call us" trigger list (3–5 bullets)
- Your contact info and a simple intro email template they can forward

**What NOT to bring:**
- A 40-slide company deck
- Generic partnership pitch
- Certification logos they've never heard of

**How to stay top of mind:**
- Monthly check-in: share 1 relevant piece of content, ask 1 question about their book
- New case study: forward it the day it's approved — "thought of [account type] on your book"
- Dreamforce / QBR: show up in person at least once a year

---

### How to Work With Snowflake PDMs / Field Reps

**The relationship dynamic:**
Snowflake field reps are measured on consumption growth — every Teqfocus implementation that drives more data through Snowflake is a win for them. Frame every interaction around consumption impact.

**Conversation starters that work:**
- "Which accounts have Snowflake deployed but underutilized — where adoption has stalled?"
- "Where are you seeing customers struggle to connect Snowflake to their Salesforce environment?"
- "What vertical is your biggest growth priority this half — Healthcare, FinServ, Telecom?"

**What to bring:**
- A joint one-pager (Teqfocus + Snowflake)
- A vertical-specific case study featuring Snowflake outcomes
- Your co-sell motion explained in 3 bullets
