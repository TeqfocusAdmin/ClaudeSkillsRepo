# MDF Justification Document — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## What MDF Is
Market Development Funds (MDF) are funds that technology partners (Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks) provide to qualified SIs and partners to co-fund joint marketing activities. MDF approvals are competitive — partners approve requests with clear ROI, credible execution plans, and alignment to their current GTM priorities.

**Rule:** MDF requests are business cases, not wish lists. Every request needs a projected pipeline number, a specific activity, and evidence that Teqfocus can execute.

---

## What Partners Fund With MDF

### Salesforce MDF Typically Funds:
- Executive Dinners and CXO Roundtables featuring Salesforce products
- Webinars and virtual events with Salesforce product content
- Digital marketing campaigns targeting Salesforce personas
- Content development (case studies, thought leadership) featuring Salesforce
- Dreamforce-adjacent events with Salesforce branding
- Sales enablement tools that support Salesforce AEs

### Snowflake MDF Typically Funds:
- Joint demand generation campaigns
- Snowflake-featured webinars and events
- Customer case study development
- Co-branded content (whitepapers, guides)
- Field marketing events in key geographies

## MDF Request Best Practices

1. **Lead with pipeline, not activity.** The approver cares about $X in partner-influenced pipeline — not how nice the venue is.
2. **Be specific about the partner product featured.** Generic "Salesforce-related event" requests get deprioritized. "Agentforce + Data Cloud webinar targeting CDOs in Healthcare" gets funded.
3. **Show your own co-investment.** MDF is matched funding — demonstrating Teqfocus' financial commitment signals execution seriousness.
4. **Align to the partner's current GTM quarter.** Ask your PDM/PBM what's top of mind for the partner this quarter — then frame your request around that.
5. **Submit early.** MDF budgets are finite and allocated quarterly. Late submissions get declined regardless of quality.
6. **Close the loop.** Post-campaign reports build the track record for the next MDF request. Missing reporting is the fastest way to lose future MDF access.
