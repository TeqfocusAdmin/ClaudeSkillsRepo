# Joint One-Pager Reference — Teqfocus Partner Marketing

## Purpose
A joint one-pager is a co-branded asset — Teqfocus + partner — designed to be used by either party's field team in front of a customer. It must work for both sides: credible from the partner's perspective, outcome-led from the customer's perspective, and clearly differentiated from what either company does alone.

---

## Joint One-Pager Structure

### Section 1: Headline
The customer outcome — not a company name announcement.

✅ *"How [Industry] Enterprises Are Connecting Their Data, AI, and CRM — With Teqfocus and Snowflake"*
✅ *"The Salesforce + Data Cloud Architecture That Makes AI Viable in [Industry]"*
❌ *"Teqfocus + Snowflake: A Powerful Technology Partnership"*
❌ *"Introducing Our Joint Solution"*

---

### Section 2: The Problem (2–3 lines)
The specific challenge a customer in this industry / persona faces that neither company solves alone.

> Template: *"[Persona] in [industry] are dealing with [specific problem]. Most [Salesforce-only / data-only] approaches don't solve it because [root cause]. What's required is [what the joint solution uniquely delivers]."*

**Why this section matters:** It frames the joint solution as an answer to a real problem — not as a partnership announcement. The customer reads this and thinks "that's exactly our situation."

---

### Section 3: The Joint Solution (3–4 bullets)
What Teqfocus + Partner deliver together. Each bullet = one specific, outcome-led thing.

**Format:** **[What we do together]** — [Why it matters to the customer]

For Teqfocus + Snowflake:
- **Unified data foundation on Snowflake** — governed, real-time, AI-ready data that actually reflects your business
- **Salesforce connected to your data layer** — so your CRM runs on operational truth, not fragmented inputs
- **AI built on both** — Teqfocus builds the ML pipeline on Snowflake, surfaces insights in Salesforce where decisions happen
- **One partner, full accountability** — no gap between your data platform and your applications

For Teqfocus + Salesforce:
- **Data Cloud + existing data infrastructure** — Teqfocus connects your Salesforce Data Cloud to where your data actually lives
- **Agentforce built on real business data** — AI agents that run on governed, industry-specific data pipelines
- **Summit-level Salesforce depth** — implementation that's built to scale, not just to go live
- **From platform to outcome** — we measure success in adoption and business impact, not go-live dates

---

### Section 4: Why This Partnership (2–3 lines)
Why Teqfocus + this partner specifically — what's unique about this combination.

> *"Teqfocus is one of the few Summit Partners that also brings certified Snowflake implementation depth — which means customers don't need to coordinate between a Salesforce SI and a data consultancy. We own the connection between both platforms and are accountable for the outcome."*

---

### Section 5: Industry Proof (1 result)
One client outcome relevant to the target industry. Anonymized if needed. Metric-led.

> *"[Industry type] enterprise — unified [X] data sources on Snowflake, connected Salesforce Data Cloud, deployed AI-powered [use case]. Result: [metric + timeframe]."* `[ADD APPROVED METRIC]`

---

### Section 6: CTA (1 line)
Specific and low-friction.

✅ *"Talk to a Teqfocus architect about your [Snowflake / Salesforce + data] roadmap → [link or name + contact]"*
✅ *"See how this architecture works in [industry] → [case study link]"*
❌ *"Contact us to learn more"*

## Joint One-Pager Variants

### Variant A: Platform + Use Case
Focus: One specific platform combination + one specific use case
Example: *"Teqfocus + Snowflake: Building AI-Ready Data Infrastructure for Healthcare"*

### Variant B: Industry + Challenge
Focus: Vertical-specific challenge that both partners help solve
Example: *"How Telecom Leaders Are Solving Churn With Snowflake + Salesforce — Implemented by Teqfocus"*

### Variant C: Agentforce / AI-Specific
Focus: AI mandate + the data foundation required
Example: *"Agentforce Works When Your Data Does — How Teqfocus Builds Both"*

### Variant D: AE Leave-Behind
Focus: Brief, internal-facing — for partner AEs to understand the co-sell motion
Example: *"When to Bring Teqfocus Into Your Deal — A Quick Reference for Salesforce AEs"*

---

## Joint One-Pager Writing Rules

- **Max 350 words of body copy** — leave room for visuals
- **Customer language throughout** — not Teqfocus language, not partner language
- **Both brands mentioned, neither dominates** — this is a peer asset
- **Never imply the partnership is new** — it's an established working relationship
- **One CTA only** — and it must be actionable, not generic
