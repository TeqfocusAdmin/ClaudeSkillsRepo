# East Bay CXO Newsletter Reference

## What This Newsletter Is
A short, community-focused newsletter sent to East Bay CXO dinner attendees
and the broader invitation list. Two purposes only:
1. Recap one moment from the last dinner
2. Announce and invite to the upcoming dinner

Nothing else. No industry insights. No Teqfocus thought leadership. No external
resources. Just the community and the next conversation.

## Writing Guide

### "From the Last Dinner" section
- One insight or exchange — not a summary
- Written as: "Someone at the table said [X] — and it changed the direction of the conversation"
- Or: "The question nobody had a clean answer to was [Y]"
- The reader should think: "I wish I'd been there"
- 2–3 sentences maximum

**Example:**
*"Last month someone asked: 'At what point does AI governance become a competitive disadvantage?' Nobody in the room had a confident answer — and that was the most honest 20 minutes we've had in a while."*

### "Coming Up" section
- Lead with the THEME — the question or tension the dinner is built around
- Why is this conversation worth having right now? Be specific.
- The date and logistics come after the narrative — never before
- The CTA is simple: RSVP link or reply

**Example:**
*"Next month we're sitting down to talk about what enterprise AI governance actually looks like inside a regulated company — not as a policy document, but as a daily operating decision. If you're wrestling with this in your organization, we'd like you in the room."*

**Never write:**
- "Teqfocus is hosting its next CXO Dinner..."
- "Join us for an evening of..."
- "We're excited to announce..."

## Subject Line Formulas

Under 50 characters. Reference the recap or the upcoming theme.

**Recap-led:**
- `"From last month's dinner — one question"`
- `"What nobody had an answer to"`
- `"The conversation that didn't end"`

**Event-led:**
- `"[Theme in 5–7 words] — [City, Month]"`
- `"The question for next month's dinner"`
- `"6 seats left — [short theme description]"`

## Key Constraints
- **Length: 150–250 words total.** This is intentionally brief.
- **Two sections only** — recap + upcoming. Never add more.
- **No Teqfocus services, case studies, or thought leadership.**
- **Warm, human tone** — host of a community, not a vendor sending an email blast.
