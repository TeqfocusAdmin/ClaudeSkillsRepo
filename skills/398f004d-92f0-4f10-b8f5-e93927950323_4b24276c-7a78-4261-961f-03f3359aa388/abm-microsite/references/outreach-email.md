# Reference: Companion Outreach Email (`email-[slug].md`)

This reference defines the format and rules for the personalized email
a senior Teqfocus person sends when the microsite goes live.

---

## Template to Generate

```markdown
# Outreach Email: [Company Name] ABM Microsite

**Send when:** Microsite is live and set to Public in WordPress
**Send from:** A named senior Teqfocus person (account owner or practice lead)
**Send to:** Primary decision-maker at [Company Name]

---

**Subject:** [SUBJECT LINE — under 50 characters, see rules below]

---

**Body:**

[FIRST NAME],

[OPENING LINE: A specific observation about their company, industry, or a trigger
event — not about Teqfocus. 1–2 sentences.]

[BRIDGE LINE: Connect the observation to a question or implication for their
business. 1 sentence.]

[VALUE LINE: What Teqfocus put together for them, framed as a point of view — not
a sales pitch. 1 sentence. Include the microsite URL here.]

[CTA LINE: One specific, low-friction ask. 1 sentence.]

[SIGN-OFF NAME]
Teqfocus
[TITLE]

---

**Microsite URL:** `teqfocus.com/abm/[slug]`
```

---

## Subject Line Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Length | Under 50 characters |
| Tone | Specific and earned — never "checking in" or "quick question" |
| No exclamation points | Ever |
| Reference something real | Company name, their industry, a trigger event, or a specific outcome |
| Formats that work | *Observation + gap* · *Named outcome* · *Specific problem* · *For [Company] specifically* |

**Good examples:**
```
"[Company]'s data layer — a different take"
"Built this for [Company] — 3 observations"
"For your Q3 AI initiative, [First Name]"
"What [Industry] CIOs are doing differently"
"[Company]'s Salesforce — where the gap is"
```

**Bad examples:**
```
"Exciting opportunity for [Company]!"         ← exclamation point, hype
"Quick question about your tech stack"        ← overused opener
"Teqfocus + [Company] = better outcomes"      ← formula, no specificity
"Following up on our conversation"            ← assumes prior contact
```

---

## Body Copy Rules

**Line 1 — Opening observation:**
- Opens with the company name or first name only — never "I hope this finds you well"
- References something specific: a known trigger, industry trend, or observable signal
- Teqfocus is not mentioned in line 1
- Example: *"Apollo Hospitals is managing patient data across 70+ facilities — and most health systems at that scale are hitting the same wall: data that exists in silos but needs to act as one."*

**Line 2 — Bridge:**
- Connects the observation to a business implication
- One sentence, specific to their situation
- Example: *"That gap is usually what stalls AI initiatives before they start."*

**Line 3 — Value framing:**
- Introduces the microsite as a point of view, not a product pitch
- Include the URL inline
- Example: *"I put together a short page with how we'd approach this for Apollo specifically: teqfocus.com/abm/apollo-hospitals"*

**Line 4 — CTA:**
- One ask. Low friction.
- Matches the `cta_type` from the intake
- Example: *"Would a 30-minute data readiness review be worth your time this month?"*

---

## Tone Reminders

- Write as a senior Teqfocus practitioner sending a considered note — not a BDR
  sending their 4th follow-up
- The microsite does the selling — the email just earns the click
- Short is better. 4–6 lines total. Not a paragraph essay.
- Never: leverage, synergy, best-in-class, holistic, cutting-edge, robust
- Never: "I wanted to reach out", "I hope this email finds you well",
         "As a leader in [industry]", "We are excited to share"
