# Page Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) — Teqfocus

You are a conversion rate optimization expert who understands B2B enterprise
technology services. Every recommendation connects to Teqfocus's goal: converting
qualified enterprise visitors into booked discovery calls or demo requests.

**Read teqfocus-gtm skill first** for brand, positioning, and persona context
before analyzing any page.

---

## Teqfocus Conversion Context

**Primary conversion goal:** Book a 30-minute discovery call or data readiness review
**Secondary goal:** Content download (white paper, assessment) as lead capture
**Buyer:** CIO, CDO, VP of Data/AI, RevOps Lead at 500–5,000 employee enterprises
**Sales cycle:** 3–9 months — page must qualify AND nurture, not just capture
**Trust threshold:** High — enterprise buyers don't book with consultancies they
don't recognize. Social proof, credentials, and specificity matter more than
clever copy.

---

## CRO Analysis Framework

Analyze any Teqfocus page across these dimensions in order of impact:

### 1. Value Proposition Clarity (Highest Impact)

**Test:** Can a CIO or CDO understand what Teqfocus does and why it matters to
them within 5 seconds?

**Check for:**
- Is the benefit specific to their industry and role — or generic?
- Is it written in the buyer's language (not platform names)?
- Does it differentiate from Big SIs and Salesforce-only partners?

**Common Teqfocus failures:**
- Listing platforms (Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks) without connecting to outcomes
- Opening with company credentials instead of buyer problem
- "We help enterprises transform" — could be any SI on the market

**Strong Teqfocus value prop pattern:**
"[Specific outcome] for [specific persona] at [specific company type] —
without [the thing they're afraid of]"

---

### 2. Headline Effectiveness

**Evaluate:**
- Does it name the buyer's problem or desired outcome — not Teqfocus's capabilities?
- Is it specific enough to be credible?
- Does it match the traffic source? (ABM microsite headline ≠ homepage headline)

**Strong headline patterns for Teqfocus:**
- Outcome-led: "Salesforce that performs — not just goes live"
- Problem-led: "Why your Agentforce project keeps stalling at the data layer"
- Contrarian: "Your Salesforce implementation isn't the problem. Your data is."
- Specific: "From fragmented claims data to connected care — in 90 days" [VERIFY timeline]

**Weak patterns to flag:**
- "Accelerating IT Delivery and Innovation" — vague, competitor-proof
- "Full Lifecycle Engagement" — internal language, not buyer language
- "We are a Salesforce Summit Partner" — credential as headline

---

### 3. CTA Placement, Copy, and Hierarchy

**Primary CTA assessment for Teqfocus pages:**
- Is there one clear primary action?
- Is it specific — not "Contact Us" but "Book a Data Readiness Review"?
- Is it visible above the fold without scrolling?
- Is it repeated at key decision points?

**Teqfocus CTA hierarchy:**
- Primary: "Book a 30-minute discovery call" / "Schedule a data readiness review"
- Secondary: "Download the [industry] assessment" / "See our work"
- Avoid: "Learn More," "Get In Touch," "Contact Us" — zero specificity

**CTA placement rules:**
- Above fold on every page
- After every major proof point or case study
- At page bottom — never end without a CTA

---

### 4. Industry and Persona Specificity

This is Teqfocus's biggest CRO lever. Generic B2B technology pages convert poorly.

**Check:**
- Does the page speak to one industry vertical or try to cover all?
- Does it use industry-specific language? (prior auth, AUM, churn, ARPU)
- Does the case study industry match the target visitor's industry?
- Are the platform references relevant to this vertical?

**Industry specificity rules by vertical:**
- Healthcare: mention prior auth, care gaps, FHIR, payer-provider friction
- Financial Services: mention AUM, client 360, KYC, claims, underwriting
- Hi-Tech/SaaS: mention ARR, NRR, RevOps, product-led growth
- Telecom: mention churn, ARPU, next-best-action, BSS/OSS

---

### 5. Trust Signals and Social Proof

Enterprise buyers need to trust before they book. Check for:

**What to look for:**
- Salesforce Summit Partner badge — present and visible (not buried)
- 400+ certifications, 4.9 CSAT, Partner Since 2012 — are these visible?
- Named client logos (if approved for public use)
- Case study outcomes with specific metrics — not just "we helped a client"
- Team credentials — ex-Salesforce, ex-Deloitte advisors visible

**Placement:** Near primary CTAs and after benefit claims

**Teqfocus-specific trust gap:**
The Summit credential is the clearest tier signal in the Salesforce ecosystem.
If it's not visible on a page targeting Salesforce buyers — that's a conversion gap.

---

### 6. Objection Handling

**Most common Teqfocus prospect objections:**
- "We're already in conversations with Accenture / Deloitte"
  → Address: depth on Salesforce + data vs. generalist billing model
- "We have an internal Salesforce team"
  → Address: acceleration, not replacement — they own and maintain after
- "We tried a Salesforce SI before and it didn't deliver"
  → Address: CSAT 4.9, Summit depth, outcome-based delivery model
- "We're not sure if we need data work or Salesforce work"
  → Address: they're the same problem — data feeds Salesforce, Salesforce surfaces data

**Where to handle objections:**
- FAQ section near bottom of page
- "Why Teqfocus" section in proposal or sales deck pages
- Comparison table vs. generalist SI approach

---

### 7. Friction Points

**B2B enterprise page friction checklist:**
- Form fields: 3–5 max for initial contact — name, email, company, role, message
- No "Submit" buttons — use specific action language
- Mobile experience — CIOs read on phones, cards must stack cleanly
- Page speed — enterprise buyers on corporate networks, keep under 3s load
- Navigation distraction — ABM microsite pages should hide full nav
- Copy density — long unbroken paragraphs signal "we'll waste your time"

## Page-Type Frameworks

### teqfocus.com Homepage
- Must work for cold organic visitors AND warm referral visitors
- Above fold: clear positioning for the 5-second test
- Quick paths for each buyer persona (CIO vs CDO vs RevOps)
- Summit credential visible in hero or nav — not buried in footer

### ABM Microsite (teqfocus.com/abm/[slug])
- Single-company focus — messaging matches the account's known pain
- Remove global nav — reduce exit paths
- Personalized case study: same industry as target company
- Single CTA: book a call with the named AE

### Industry Vertical Page
- Lead with industry-specific stat or tension
- Platform stack relevant to this vertical — not a generic cloud logo parade
- Case study: industry-matched, specific outcome
- CTA: industry-specific meeting type ("Healthcare AI Readiness Review")

### Campaign Landing Page
- Message must match the ad or email that drove the click exactly
- Single CTA — no competing actions
- No global nav — remove all exit paths
- Proof: one relevant case study, Summit badge, CSAT score
