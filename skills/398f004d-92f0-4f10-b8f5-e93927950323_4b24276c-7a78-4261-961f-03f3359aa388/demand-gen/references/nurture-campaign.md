# Nurture Email Campaign Reference — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
Prospects who have engaged with Teqfocus content (downloaded a resource, attended a webinar, listened to TeqTalk, visited a key page) but have not booked a meeting. They are warm — but not sales-ready yet.

**Sequence Length:** 4–6 emails depending on entry point
**Cadence:** 5–7 days between emails (slower than outbound — let them breathe)
**Goal:** Build trust and authority → surface the right problem → convert to a meeting when timing is right

---

## Nurture vs. Outbound: Key Differences

| Element | Outbound | Nurture |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect knows Teqfocus? | No / barely | Yes — engaged once |
| Tone | Curiosity-led, slightly bolder | Insight-led, peer-to-peer |
| Credential intro | Email 3 | Can appear earlier if natural |
| Meeting ask | Email 3–4 | Email 4–6 (earn the right first) |
| Content references | Minimal | Active — link to resources |
| Personalization | Role + Industry | Role + Industry + Specific content they engaged with |

## Nurture Sequence by Entry Point

---

### Entry Point A: Content Download (Blog, Guide, One-Pager)

**Email 1 — The Acknowledgment + Depth Add**
- Subject: Extends the topic they downloaded — gives them more, not a pitch
- Body: 2–3 lines on a related insight or a "what most people miss" angle on the same topic
- CTA: Link to a related piece or a follow-up resource

```
Subject: One more thing on [topic of download]
Preview: [Specific angle they may not have considered]

[Name], most people who read [resource title] focus on [common 
angle]. The part that tends to matter more in practice is 
[specific insight that goes deeper].

Here's a piece that goes there: [link]

If you're working through [specific challenge], happy to 
have a short conversation about how [industry type] teams 
are navigating it.

[First name]
```

**Email 2 — The Problem Deepener**
- Reference the problem the content addressed — go one layer deeper
- 1 specific stat or observation `[VERIFY]`
- CTA: Another resource or soft "is this relevant to your situation?" close

**Email 3 — The Proof**
- One client story relevant to the topic they engaged with
- Industry-matched if possible
- CTA: "Would it be useful to see how we approached this?"

**Email 4 — The Ask**
- Direct but low-friction meeting ask
- Frame as a specific session type, not a generic demo
- CTA: Calendar link

---

### Entry Point B: TeqTalk Listener / Newsletter Subscriber

**Email 1 — The Episode Tie-In**
- Reference the specific episode or newsletter issue they engaged with
- Pull the sharpest insight from that episode and extend it
- No pitch — pure value

```
Subject: [Sharp insight from the episode they engaged with]
Preview: [Why this matters for their role/industry]

[Name], the part of [guest name]'s conversation on TeqTalk 
that I keep thinking about is [specific insight in 1 line].

[2 lines: why this is harder than it looks in practice, 
 or what it implies for [their industry/role]]

There's a related piece on this here if it's useful: [link]

[First name]
```

**Email 2 — The Pattern**
- Bring in a second data point or perspective (different episode, blog, or case study) on the same theme
- Start building a POV, not just sharing content

**Email 3 — The Proof Point**
- How Teqfocus operationalizes the insight the guest discussed
- One client example — outcome-led

**Email 4 — The Session Offer**
- Frame a session that directly connects to the theme they've been engaging with
- *"Given your interest in [topic], a [session name] might be the most useful next step"*
- Calendar link

## Long-Term Nurture: Monthly Re-Engagement Emails

For prospects who haven't converted after the initial sequence, move to a monthly re-engagement cadence:

**Monthly touch formula:**
- 1 piece of relevant content (new case study, TeqTalk episode, blog)
- 1 industry observation relevant to their role
- Soft CTA — "Worth a conversation?" or resource link
- Never pressure — this is staying top of mind, not chasing

**Subject line formulas for re-engagement:**
- *"New from TeqTalk: [episode topic relevant to them]"*
- *"[Industry] in [current quarter]: what's changing"*
- *"Thought of you when we published this"*

---

## Nurture Copy Rules

- **Always reference what they engaged with** in Email 1 — shows you're paying attention
- **Don't re-pitch what they already downloaded** — go deeper or go adjacent
- **Cadence is slower than outbound** — patience is part of the strategy
- **Never guilt-trip** for not responding — maintain peer-to-peer tone throughout
- **Every email should stand alone** as useful — if they only open one, it should still be worth their time
