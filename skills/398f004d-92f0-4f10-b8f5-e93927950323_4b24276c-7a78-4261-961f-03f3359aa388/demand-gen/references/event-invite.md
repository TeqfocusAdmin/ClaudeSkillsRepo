# Event Invite Sequence Reference — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
Driving registrations and attendance for Teqfocus-hosted or co-hosted events — including Dreamforce meetups (Happy Hour, Executive Dinner, Lunch & Learn), CXO Roundtables, regional meetups, and partner events.

---

## Event Types & Positioning

| Event Format | Audience | Tone | Primary Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive Dinner** | CIO / CDO / VP level | Exclusive, peer-level | Pipeline + relationship |
| **Happy Hour** | Practitioner + manager level | Casual, community-feel | Brand + warm pipeline |
| **Lunch & Learn** | Technical buyers + ops leaders | Educational + practical | Consideration + MQL |
| **CXO Roundtable** | C-suite only | Intimate, invitation-only | High-intent pipeline |
| **Dreamforce Meetup** | Mixed — Salesforce ecosystem | Ecosystem-native | Brand + partner leverage |

## Pre-Event Invite Sequence (3 Emails)

---

### Invite Email 1 — The First Touch (4–6 weeks before event)

**Goal:** Plant the flag. Create awareness. Don't oversell — let the event concept do the work.

**For Executive Dinner / CXO Roundtable:**
```
Subject: Dinner with [X] [industry] leaders — [City], [Date]
Preview: Invitation-only, 12 seats — details inside

[Name], we're hosting a private dinner in [City] on [Date] 
for a small group of [industry] [CIO/CDO/VP] leaders.

The conversation: [topic in 1 line — sharp and specific, 
not generic "AI transformation"].

No vendor pitches. No slides. Just a peer-level dinner with 
people navigating the same problems.

We have [X] seats. If this sounds relevant, I'd like to 
hold one for you: [registration link or "reply to confirm"]

[First name]
```

**For Happy Hour / Dreamforce Meetup:**
```
Subject: [Teqfocus] at Dreamforce — join us [Date/Time]
Preview: [Venue name], [neighborhood] — [X] mins from [landmark]

[Name], we're hosting a Happy Hour during Dreamforce:

📅 [Date] · [Time]
📍 [Venue], [Address / Neighborhood]

If you're attending Dreamforce and want to connect with 
[industry / Salesforce / data] leaders in a more human 
setting than the conference floor — we'd love to have you.

[Registration link]

[First name]
```

---

### Invite Email 2 — The Reason to Come (10–14 days before)

**Goal:** Give them a specific reason to attend beyond the format itself. Speaker, topic, or who else will be there.

```
Subject: Who's coming to [event name] + why it matters
Preview: [Speaker name or "a CDO from [industry type]"] will be there

[Name], a quick update on [event name] on [Date]:

[1–2 lines: speaker announcement, confirmed attendee profile, 
or the specific discussion topic — whatever is most compelling]

[Why this matters to them specifically — 1 line connecting 
to their role/industry]

[X] spots remain: [registration link]

[First name]
```

---

### Invite Email 3 — The Last Call (3–5 days before)

**Goal:** Urgency without pressure. Close the stragglers.

```
Subject: [X] spots left — [event name], [Date]
Preview: [Venue / city] — [Date]

[Name], [event name] is [X] days away.

We have [X] spots remaining — if you're planning to join, 
now is the time to confirm: [registration link]

[1 line: what they'll walk away with or who they'll meet]

See you there.

[First name]
```

## Post-Event Follow-Up Sequence (3 Emails)

---

### Post-Event Email 1 — The Same-Day or Next-Morning Send

**Goal:** Strike while the relationship is warm. Deliver value, open the next conversation.

```
Subject: Good seeing you at [event name]
Preview: [The thing you talked about / promised to send]

[Name], great to [meet / reconnect] at [event name].

[1 personal line if possible — reference something specific 
from the conversation, or a general observation about the event]

[If a resource was promised: "Here's [the thing I mentioned]": link]

Given what we discussed around [topic], I'd like to set up 
a more focused 30 minutes to [specific thing].

[Calendar link] — or just reply and we'll sort a time.

[First name]
```

---

### Post-Event Email 2 — The Value Add (Day 4–5)

**Goal:** Stay relevant with a piece of content connected to what was discussed at the event.

- Reference the event topic or a moment from the event conversation
- Connect to a relevant resource (case study, TeqTalk episode, blog)
- Soft CTA — "thought this might be relevant" or a question
- Do not repeat the meeting ask from Email 1

---

### Post-Event Email 3 — The Meeting Close (Day 9–12)

**Goal:** Direct, specific, low-friction meeting ask.

- Frame it as a natural continuation of the event conversation
- Name a specific session type — not a generic call
- Include calendar link
- If no response to Emails 1 and 2, acknowledge it lightly and keep it short

```
Subject: Following up — [specific session offer]
Preview: [What they'd take away from the session]

[Name], I wanted to follow up one more time on [topic 
discussed at event or theme of the event].

If it's relevant to what you're working through right now, 
I'd suggest a [session name] — [1-line description of what 
it covers and what they'd take away].

[Calendar link]

If timing isn't right, no problem — I'll be in touch 
when something relevant comes up.

[First name]
```

## Dreamforce-Specific Invite Rules

- **Reference Dreamforce explicitly** — people have Dreamforce on the brain; make the connection easy
- **Venue matters** — include neighborhood + landmark proximity. Conference attendees are navigating a city.
- **Keep logistics tight** — Date, time, location in the first 3 lines of every invite
- **No-pitch guarantee** works well for executive formats — state it directly
- **Registration should be frictionless** — form with 2–3 fields max, or a reply-to-confirm setup
- **Day-of reminder** — send a practical logistics email morning-of (address, parking, what to expect)
