# Reference: Lead Magnets — Teqfocus

Use when designing gated content assets to capture qualified leads.
Lead magnets work best when they solve a specific problem the buyer
has right now — not just interesting content.

---

## Teqfocus Lead Magnet Types

| Type | Best for | Production effort |
|---|---|---|
| Data Readiness Assessment | CDOs evaluating AI readiness | Low (questionnaire + scoring) |
| Industry Benchmark Report | CIOs benchmarking their stack | Medium (requires data) |
| Implementation Checklist | Buyers in evaluation stage | Low |
| White Paper / Framework | Awareness stage, thought leadership | High |
| ROI Calculator | Decision stage, justifying investment | Medium |
| Vertical Guide | Industry-specific buyers | Medium |

---

## Highest-Value Teqfocus Lead Magnets to Build

**1. Data Readiness Assessment (Priority 1)**
- "Is your data ready for AI?" — 10-question self-assessment
- Output: score + personalized recommendations
- Why it works: Surfaces the exact problem Teqfocus solves, positions them as the expert, creates a natural follow-up conversation
- CTA: "Book a call to review your results with a Teqfocus data architect"

**2. Healthcare AI Readiness Guide**
- "5 things healthcare CIOs must address before launching Agentforce"
- Persona: Healthcare CIO/CDO
- Why it works: Industry-specific, tied to urgent buyer concern

**3. Salesforce ROI Framework**
- "How to build the business case for your next Salesforce investment"
- Persona: CIO / VP IT making budget decisions
- Why it works: Decision-stage asset, positions Teqfocus as trusted advisor

**4. Agentforce Implementation Checklist**
- "The 12 data and integration questions to answer before going live with Agentforce"
- Persona: CDO / Salesforce admin lead
- Why it works: Immediately useful, surfaces gaps Teqfocus can fix

## Lead Magnet Landing Page Structure

**Headline:** What they get + who it's for + how fast
"Get the Healthcare AI Readiness Guide — for CIOs preparing to launch AI in 2025"

**3 bullets:** What they'll learn / what problem it solves
**Social proof:** "Used by CDOs at [industry] organizations" (or Summit badge)

**Form fields (maximum 4):**
- First name
- Work email (required)
- Company (required)
- Role/title

**CTA button:** "Get the [specific asset name]" — not "Download Now" or "Submit"

**After submission:**
- Immediate delivery (email or on-page)
- Follow-up sequence: 3-touch nurture tied to the asset topic (use lifecycle-nurture skill)
- HubSpot: tag contact with asset downloaded + lead source

## Rules
- Never gate something that doesn't solve a real problem — it won't convert or build trust
- Always deliver immediately — delayed delivery loses trust
- Tag every download in HubSpot for follow-up sequencing
- American English throughout
