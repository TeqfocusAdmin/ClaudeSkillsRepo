# Reference: Proposal / RFP Response

Use when a prospect has issued an RFP or requested a formal proposal.
Length: 8–15 pages depending on scope | Format: .docx via teqfocus-design skill

## Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (1 page)
- The problem Teqfocus understands the client to have (in their language)
- The approach Teqfocus recommends — named and specific
- The outcome the client can expect — with timeline
- Why Teqfocus over other options — 3 bullet differentiators
- Single CTA: next step to move forward

### 2. Understanding of the Problem (1–2 pages)
- Restate the client's challenge in their words — show you listened
- Underlying causes — go 2 levels deeper than the surface request
- Business impact of the status quo — cost of inaction
- Note: if RFP language is available, mirror it precisely here

### 3. Proposed Solution (2–3 pages)
- Named approach: "The Teqfocus [X] Framework" or "Our [Phase] approach"
- Phase breakdown:
  - Phase 1: [Name] — [Duration] — [Key deliverable] — [Outcome]
  - Phase 2: [Name] — [Duration] — [Key deliverable] — [Outcome]
  - Phase 3: [Name] — [Duration] — [Key deliverable] — [Outcome]
- Technology stack: which platforms, why each, how they connect
- What Teqfocus brings that others can't (Summit credential naturally here)

### 4. Proof / Relevant Experience (1–2 pages)
- 2 case studies — industry-matched to the client
- Format: Challenge → Solution → Business Outcomes
- Verified metrics only — [VERIFY] flag if uncertain
- Team credentials relevant to this engagement

### 5. Team and Engagement Model (1 page)
- Named team members and their relevant experience
- Engagement model: how Teqfocus works (Architect → Engineer → Build → Maintain)
- Communication cadence and escalation path
- Knowledge transfer approach

### 6. Investment (1 page)
- Pricing model — fixed / T&M / outcome-based
- What's included and explicitly what's not
- Milestone-based payment structure if applicable
- Note: never include pricing in first draft without client guidance

### 7. Why Teqfocus (1 page)
- Not a credential dump — frame as a match argument
- "You need [X] — here's why that's our specific strength"
- 3 proof points matched to the client's stated priorities
- Summit Partnership surfaces naturally here — not as a headline

### 8. Next Steps (half page)
- Single proposed action: "30-minute scoping call on [date options]"
- Named contact from Teqfocus
- Response deadline (if applicable)

## Voice Rules
- Write in second person for the client: "your team", "your data"
- Never "we are pleased to submit" — just submit the proposal, get to the point
- Every capability statement connected to a client outcome
- American English throughout
