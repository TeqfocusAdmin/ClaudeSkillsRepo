# One-Pager Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## When One-Pagers Are Used
- Outbound prospecting (email attachment or LinkedIn DM)
- Event and conference leave-behinds
- Post-meeting follow-ups to reinforce the pitch
- Partner co-selling collateral
- SDR / BDR outreach enablement

---

## One-Pager Template Structure

### Headline
Outcome-first. Names the result or the problem the reader recognizes.
- ✅ *"Why Healthcare CIOs Are Rethinking Their Data + Salesforce Strategy"*
- ✅ *"How Mid-Market Sales Teams Are Getting More From Salesforce in 90 Days"*
- ❌ *"Teqfocus + Snowflake: A Powerful Partnership"*

---

### Section 1 — The Problem (2–3 lines)
The specific pain this persona feels. Industry-specific language. Make it feel written for them — not recycled from a generic deck.

> Template: *"[Persona] in [industry] are [experiencing specific problem] — which means [business consequence they care about]. Most [solutions tried] haven't solved it because [root cause]."*

---

### Section 2 — The Teqfocus Approach (3–4 bullets)
How we solve it. Outcome-first phrasing. Each bullet = one clear thing we do and why it matters.

Format: **[What we do]** — [why it matters to them]

Examples:
- **Unify your data layer before building AI** — so your models run on real, governed data, not fragments
- **Connect Salesforce to your existing data infrastructure** — so your CRM reflects operational reality
- **Deploy Agentforce workflows specific to your industry** — so automation fits how your team actually works

---

### Section 3 — Why Teqfocus (2–3 bullets)
Keep it sharp. This is the credibility anchor — not a capabilities list.

Must include:
- Salesforce Summit Partner signal (framed as risk reduction, not a badge)
- Full-stack depth (Data + AI + Application — one partner)
- Industry vertical expertise (one specific industry reference if the one-pager is vertical-specific)

---

### Section 4 — Proof Point (1–2 lines)
One concrete result. Use an approved metric if available.
If not: use `[ADD APPROVED METRIC: industry]` — do not fabricate.

> *"A [industry] client reduced [metric] by [X%] within [timeframe] after implementing [solution]."*

---

### Section 5 — CTA (1 line, specific)
Never "contact us to learn more."

Options by use case:
- Prospecting: *"Book a 30-minute architecture review → [link]"*
- Event: *"Scan to see how we've done this in [industry]"*
- Post-meeting: *"Let's scope a [specific first step] — [name] will follow up by [date]"*
- Partner: *"Co-sell with us → See our joint GTM kit"*

---

## One-Pager Writing Rules

- **Max 400 words of body copy** — every line must earn its place
- **One headline, one message, one CTA** — no sprawl
- **Industry AND persona specific** — a Healthcare CIO one-pager and a Telecom CDO one-pager are completely different documents
- **Never swap logos** on a generic template and call it industry-specific
- Flag all visual needs clearly:
  - `[DESIGN FLAG: architecture diagram — Data → AI → Application layer]`
  - `[DESIGN FLAG: stat callout box]`
  - `[DESIGN FLAG: partner logo strip — Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Tableau]`
  - `[DESIGN FLAG: industry icon or hero image]`

## One-Pager Variants by Use Case

### Variant A: Persona + Industry (Most Common)
Focus: Single persona, single industry, single problem
Example title: *"How Telecom CDOs Are Building AI-Ready Data Infrastructure With Snowflake + Salesforce"*

### Variant B: Service Line
Focus: One service (e.g., Managed Services AMS, Agentforce deployment, Snowflake modernization)
Example title: *"Your Salesforce Shouldn't Need a Full Rebuild Every 18 Months"*

### Variant C: Event / Campaign Leave-Behind
Focus: Tied to a specific event theme, session, or campaign message
Example title: *"[Event Name] Exclusive: The Multi-Cloud Architecture Powering Enterprise AI in 2025"*

### Variant D: Competitive Displacement
Focus: Buyers currently using a competitor or single-layer partner
Example title: *"Getting More From Salesforce When Your Current Partner Only Knows One Layer"*
