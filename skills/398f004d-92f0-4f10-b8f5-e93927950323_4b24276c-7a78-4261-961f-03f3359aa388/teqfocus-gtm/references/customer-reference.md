# Reference: Customer Reference / Testimonial Letter

Use when an existing client is willing to provide a written reference.
Length: 200–400 words | Format: Letter on client letterhead (they finalize/sign)
Teqfocus drafts for client review — they edit and approve before use.

## Structure

### Opening
- Client author's name, title, company
- Context: how long they've worked with Teqfocus, what the engagement covered

### The Challenge (2–3 sentences)
- The specific problem before Teqfocus
- What they had tried before (optional)
- The stakes — why it mattered

### The Engagement (3–4 sentences)
- What Teqfocus did — specific, not generic
- How the team worked (responsiveness, expertise, approach)
- Notable moment or quality that stood out

### The Outcome (2–3 sentences)
- Measurable result — verified with the client before including
- Business impact — not just technical delivery
- What changed for the team or organization

### Closing
- Recommendation statement: would they work with Teqfocus again / refer others
- Contact offer: willing to speak with prospects (if agreed)

### Sign-off
- Client name, title, company
- Date

## Rules
- Draft in the client's voice — not Teqfocus's voice
- Every metric confirmed by the client before inclusion
- Never include metrics the client hasn't explicitly approved
- Submit draft to client clearly marked "DRAFT — FOR YOUR REVIEW AND APPROVAL"
- American English throughout (unless client is in a non-US English-speaking market)
