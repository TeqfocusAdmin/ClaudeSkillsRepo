# Case Study Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## Case Study Structure (3-Part Format)

Every Teqfocus case study follows this structure — no exceptions.

---

### Part 1: Challenge
**Goal:** Make the reader feel the weight of the problem before Teqfocus enters the story.

**What to include:**
- Client context (industry, company type, size — no name unless approved)
- 2–3 specific problem areas, each with a business consequence
- The stakes: what happens if this isn't solved?

**Writing rule:** Use the client's world and language. Avoid Teqfocus-centric framing here. This section should read like the client wrote it.

**Format:**
> *"A [industry] organization serving [X customers / employees / patients] was facing [specific problem]. [Business consequence]. Additionally, [second problem area leading to third consequence]. Manual processes and fragmented data meant [operational impact]."*

---

### Part 2: Solution
**Goal:** Show the intelligence of the approach — not just the tools used.

**What to include:**
- Why Teqfocus' approach was different (start with design thinking, not platform selection)
- The architecture or workflow built — described in outcome terms, not technical spec
- How the multi-cloud stack served the specific use case (Salesforce + Snowflake + Databricks + etc.)
- Any accelerators, pre-built components, or industry-specific frameworks used

**Writing rule:** Platforms are mentioned to give credibility — not as the main story. The story is: *"We understood the problem deeply, designed for the outcome, and used the right tools to deliver it."*

**Format:**
> *"Teqfocus began with a [discovery/assessment/design sprint] to [understand root cause / map data flows / align stakeholders]. We designed a [solution description] built on [relevant stack], enabling [outcome A] and [outcome B]."*

---

### Part 3: Business Outcomes
**Goal:** Let the numbers close the story.

**What to include:**
- 2–4 quantified outcomes (%, $, time saved, risk reduced, adoption rate)
- 1 qualitative outcome if it's compelling (e.g., "Sales team now trusts the forecast for the first time")
- Optional: A brief client quote if approved

**Writing rule:** Outcomes go first — before explanation. Big number in bold or callout box. `[DESIGN FLAG: metric callout]`

**Format:**
> *"Following implementation, the client achieved: [Metric 1], [Metric 2], [Metric 3]. For the first time, [qualitative shift in how the business operates]."*

---

## Case Study Writing Rules

- **400–600 words** for full case study. 150–200 words for a condensed proof slide version.
- **No client name unless approved** — use "[Fortune 500 Healthcare Provider]" or "[Regional Telecom Operator]"
- **Every case study must have at least one metric** — if unavailable, use `[ADD APPROVED METRIC]`
- **No fabricated stats** — ever. Use `[VERIFY]` for anything unconfirmed.
- **Industry-specific language throughout** — healthcare case studies sound like healthcare, telecom like telecom
- **Never** open with "Teqfocus helped a client..." — open with the client's problem
