# Outbound Email Sequence Reference — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
Cold or warm outbound to a net-new prospect. They have not engaged with Teqfocus content yet — or they engaged once (e.g., downloaded a resource) but have not taken a meeting.

**Sequence Length:** 5 emails
**Cadence:** Email 1 → Day 3 → Day 6 → Day 11 → Day 16
**Goal:** Book a meeting — architecture review, readiness assessment, or intro call

---

## The 5-Email Outbound Structure

---

### Email 1 — The Insight Open

**Goal:** Get the open. Establish relevance. Do not pitch. Do not mention Teqfocus services.

**Structure:**
- Subject: Industry-specific observation or named problem (under 50 chars)
- Preview: Extends the subject — does not repeat it
- Opening line: Sharp industry observation specific to their role
- Body (2 short paragraphs): Expand the observation → surface the tension it creates
- CTA: Soft — a question or a "worth a conversation?" close

**Subject line formulas:**
- *"The data problem blocking most [industry] AI rollouts"*
- *"Why [industry] Salesforce builds underperform in year 2"*
- *"[Persona] at [industry] companies are hitting the same wall"*

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't name Teqfocus in Email 1
- Don't list services or platforms
- Don't use "I wanted to reach out" or "Hope this finds you well"

**Template:**
```
Subject: [Industry-specific problem — under 50 chars]
Preview: [What makes this specific to their situation]

[Sharp opening line — industry observation, no pleasantry]

[2–3 lines: why this problem is more expensive than it looks, 
 or why the common fix doesn't actually work]

[1 line: genuine question that makes them think — not a pitch]

[First name]
```

---

### Email 2 — The Problem Frame

**Goal:** Name the specific problem they're likely experiencing. Make them feel understood before offering anything.

**Structure:**
- Subject: The specific problem, named directly
- Body: "Most [persona] in [industry] we talk to are dealing with [specific problem]" → 2 lines on why it persists → 1 line bridging to Teqfocus without pitching
- CTA: Link to a relevant resource (case study, blog, or one-pager)

**Subject line formulas:**
- *"What [persona] teams usually discover too late"*
- *"The [specific problem] most [industry] teams underestimate"*
- *"A pattern we keep seeing in [industry]"*

**Template:**
```
Subject: [Named problem — under 50 chars]
Preview: [Specific consequence of the problem]

Most [persona] we speak with in [industry] are running into 
the same issue: [specific, concrete problem in 1 line].

[2 lines: why it persists — usually a systemic or architectural reason, 
 not a skills gap. Keep it observational, not judgmental.]

We've been working through this with [industry type] teams — 
here's a piece that maps the problem well: [resource link]

Worth a read?

[First name]
```

---

### Email 3 — The Proof Point

**Goal:** Introduce Teqfocus with evidence, not claims. This is the first email where Teqfocus is named directly.

**Structure:**
- Subject: Outcome-led — what was achieved, not who Teqfocus is
- Body: One client story in 3 sentences (industry type → problem → outcome → Teqfocus role)
- Surface Summit credential naturally here — not as a headline
- CTA: "Want to see how we'd approach this for [their company/industry]?"

**Subject line formulas:**
- *"How a [industry] team cut [metric] by [X]%"*
- *"[Outcome] in [timeframe] — what made it work"*
- *"A [industry] case worth 3 minutes"*

**Template:**
```
Subject: [Outcome achieved — under 50 chars]
Preview: [The specific thing that made it possible]

A [industry type] client came to us with [specific problem].
[What Teqfocus built — architecture in 1 line, no platform stacking].
[The outcome — metric + timeframe] [VERIFY]

We're a Salesforce Summit Partner with a dedicated [industry] 
practice — which means we've seen this problem across enough 
implementations to know what actually works.

Would it be useful to walk through how we'd approach this 
for [company name / their industry]?

[First name]
```

---

### Email 4 — The Differentiator

**Goal:** Address the "why Teqfocus over a different SI" question before they ask it.

**Structure:**
- Subject: Addresses the comparison or the "why you" hesitation
- Body: What makes Teqfocus different — Summit depth + full-stack (data + AI + app) + industry IP. One specific proof. Short.
- CTA: Book a defined session — "30-minute [session name]"

**Subject line formulas:**
- *"Why most SIs can't do both — data and Salesforce"*
- *"Full-stack vs. point solution — the tradeoff worth knowing"*
- *"What Summit Partnership actually means for your project"*

**Template:**
```
Subject: [Differentiator angle — under 50 chars]
Preview: [The specific gap most SIs leave]

Most Salesforce partners stop at the CRM layer. Most data 
consultancies don't touch the application layer.

Teqfocus works across both — which means AI projects don't 
stall because the data layer wasn't ready, and Salesforce 
implementations don't underperform because the data feeding 
them wasn't connected.

As a Summit Partner with a dedicated [vertical] practice, 
we bring [specific IP or accelerator if available] that 
compresses time-to-value significantly.

I'd like to offer a 30-minute [session name] — no pitch, 
just a structured conversation about where your roadmap 
stands and where the gaps tend to show up.

[Calendar link]

[First name]
```

---

### Email 5 — The Break-Up

**Goal:** One last value add + honest close. No guilt. No pressure. Leave the door open.

**Structure:**
- Subject: Honest and direct — acknowledge the silence without passive aggression
- Body: Share one final resource or insight. Close without pressure.
- CTA: Resource link OR calendar link — give them a choice

**Subject line formulas:**
- *"Last one from me (for now)"*
- *"Leaving you with this"*
- *"One more thing before I step back"*

**Template:**
```
Subject: Last one from me (for now)
Preview: [One line on what you're leaving them with]

I'll keep this short — I know your inbox doesn't need 
another email.

If the timing isn't right for a conversation, no problem. 
I'll check back in [timeframe].

Before I do — here's [resource: article / case study / 
guide] that's relevant to what [persona] teams in [industry] 
are navigating right now: [link]

If anything resonates and you want to talk, my calendar 
is here: [link]

[First name]
```

## Sequence Customization by Persona

### CIO / CTO Sequence
- Email 1 hook: Integration architecture or failed AI initiative observation
- Email 3 proof: Architecture complexity handled across cloud + Salesforce
- Meeting ask: "Architecture alignment session"

### CDO / Data Leader Sequence
- Email 1 hook: AI stalling at the data layer / failed POC observation
- Email 3 proof: Data + AI implementation outcome with measurable adoption
- Meeting ask: "Data readiness review"

### RevOps / VP Sales Sequence
- Email 1 hook: Salesforce forecast accuracy or CRM adoption problem
- Email 3 proof: CRM optimization outcome — adoption rate + pipeline visibility
- Meeting ask: "CRM health check"
