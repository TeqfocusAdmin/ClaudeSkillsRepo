# LinkedIn DM / InMail Scripts — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
First-touch or warm outreach on LinkedIn — for prospects who haven't entered an email sequence yet, or as a parallel channel to email. Also used for follow-ups after a prospect engages with a LinkedIn post.

---

## LinkedIn vs. Email: What Changes

| Element | Email | LinkedIn DM |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 150–250 words | 50–100 words max |
| Tone | Professional peer | More casual, human |
| CTA | Calendar link or resource | Conversation or quick question |
| Personalization | Role + Industry | Role + specific post/content they engaged with |
| Best use | Sequence touchpoints | First touch + post-engagement follow-up |

---

## Cold Connection Request Note (300 character limit)

**Goal:** Get the connection accepted. Nothing more. Do not pitch.

**Formula:** [Relevance signal] + [Why connecting makes sense] + [No ask]

```
[Name], I work with [industry type] [CIO/CDO/data] leaders 
on [general topic area]. Your work at [company] caught my 
eye — thought it'd be worth connecting.
```

```
[Name], I host TeqTalk — a podcast with [industry] AI and 
data leaders. Your background in [specific thing] is exactly 
the kind of perspective we cover. Worth connecting.
```

---

## First DM After Connection Accepted

**Goal:** Start a conversation — not deliver a pitch. Short. Human.

**Send within 24–48 hours of connection accepted.**

```
[Name], thanks for connecting.

I work with [industry] [persona] teams on [specific problem 
area in 1 line]. It's a topic that comes up constantly in 
the conversations we're having — curious if it's on your 
radar too.

[First name]
```

```
[Name], appreciate the connection.

We just published a piece on [topic relevant to their role 
and industry] that I thought might be worth your time: 
[link]

Happy to swap notes if [topic] is something you're navigating.

[First name]
```

## Post-Engagement DM (They Liked/Commented on a Teqfocus Post)

**Goal:** Turn a content interaction into a conversation. Reference what they engaged with.

```
[Name], saw you [liked / commented on] our post about 
[topic] — glad it resonated.

[1 line: a slightly deeper angle on the same topic]

Is this something you're actively working through at 
[company]?

[First name]
```

---

## InMail (For Premium Outreach — Non-Connections)

**Goal:** Same as cold email — establish relevance, start a conversation, no pitch.
**Length:** 100 words max. LinkedIn InMail that looks like email gets ignored.

```
Subject: [Industry-specific problem — sharp, under 8 words]

[Name], [1-line industry observation specific to their role].

[2–3 lines: the tension this creates / why the common fix 
doesn't work / what's changing].

Happy to share how [industry type] teams are approaching 
this differently — worth a quick conversation?

[First name]
[Title], Teqfocus
```

---

## Follow-Up DM (After No Response to Email)

**Goal:** A lighter-weight channel to re-surface the conversation.

```
[Name], sent you a note recently about [topic] — wasn't 
sure if it landed in your inbox.

Thought a shorter version might be easier here: [1-line 
version of the insight or question].

Worth a conversation?
```

---

## LinkedIn DM Rules

- **Never pitch on the first message** — the connection note and first DM exist only to start a conversation
- **Reference their content, company, or role specifically** — anything generic gets ignored
- **Keep it under 100 words** — if it looks like an email, it won't get read like a DM
- **One question or CTA only** — don't give them 3 things to respond to
- **Don't paste an email sequence into LinkedIn** — rewrite it for the channel
- **No emojis in outreach to C-suite** — casual is fine; informal is fine; unprofessional is not
- **Follow up once, maximum twice** — LinkedIn is not an email inbox
