# Case Study Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Purpose
Case studies are the highest-credibility asset in the Teqfocus content mix. They are used for sales enablement, partner marketing, and thought leadership. Every case study must tell a story — not just list features and outcomes.

---

## Standard Case Study Format (3-Part Structure)

### Part 1: Challenge
- Clearly define 2–3 specific problem areas
- Use the client's industry context — not generic "digital transformation" language
- Name the business impact of the problem (cost, risk, missed revenue, inefficiency)
- Write from the client's perspective — not Teqfocus'

**What to include:**
- Industry and company profile (anonymized if needed)
- Specific pain points — with business consequence, not just technical description
- What was tried before (if applicable) — shows the problem is non-trivial
- Why this was urgent or strategically important

**Example framing:**
> "A regional healthcare network was managing patient risk stratification across 6 disparate EMR systems. Clinicians had no unified view of SDOH factors, leading to reactive care and avoidable ER visits — costing the system an estimated $X annually. [VERIFY]"

---

### Part 2: Solution
- AI-driven, workflow-automated, and integrated data approaches
- Show the architecture decision — not just the platform list
- Explain WHY this approach was chosen (speed, compliance, existing stack, scalability)
- Highlight what Teqfocus did that a generic SI couldn't

**What to include:**
- The architecture built (data layer → AI/analytics → application)
- Key platforms used and why (not just a logo list)
- Teqfocus-specific contribution: IP, accelerators, methodology, expertise
- Timeline and implementation approach (phased, MVP-first, etc.)
- Any governance, compliance, or change management elements

**Example framing:**
> "Teqfocus built a unified patient data layer on Snowflake, integrating all 6 EMR sources via MuleSoft APIs. A Databricks ML pipeline scored patient risk daily based on SDOH indicators and clinical history. Clinicians accessed insights directly in Salesforce Health Cloud — without leaving their existing workflow."

---

### Part 3: Business Outcomes
- Measurable impact only — no vague statements
- Prioritize: cost reduction, revenue impact, time saved, adoption rate, risk reduced
- Include both leading and lagging indicators where possible
- Flag unverified numbers with `[VERIFY]`

**What to include:**
- 3–5 specific metrics with timeframe
- Before/after contrast where possible
- Qualitative outcome (clinical confidence, team adoption, exec visibility) if quantitative is limited
- Quote from client stakeholder if available (or placeholder)

**Preferred metric formats:**
- "Reduced avoidable ER visits by X% within 6 months [VERIFY]"
- "Decreased manual reporting time from X hours/week to Y [VERIFY]"
- "Achieved 90%+ Salesforce adoption within 60 days of go-live [VERIFY]"
- "Identified $XM in at-risk renewals in the first quarter post-launch [VERIFY]"

## Case Study Voice Rules

- **Tell the client's story** — Teqfocus is the guide, not the hero
- **Name the industry pain specifically** — no generic "improving efficiency" language
- **Architecture = the WHY, not just the WHAT** — explain decisions, not just outcomes
- **Every metric needs a timeframe** — "reduced by 30%" means nothing without "in 90 days"
- **Short paragraphs** — 2–4 lines max per paragraph
- **Active voice throughout**
