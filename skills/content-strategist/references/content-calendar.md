# Content Calendar Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Calendar Purpose
The content calendar is the operational layer of Teqfocus' omnichannel content engine. It connects four content pillars — Podcast (TeqTalk), Use Cases, Webinar, and CXO Meetup — into a weekly publishing rhythm that drives MQLs and brand visibility.

---

## The Four Content Pillars

| Pillar | Cadence | Primary Output | MQL Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TeqTalk Podcast** | Bi-weekly episodes | Newsletter, LinkedIn posts, Reels, YouTube | Awareness → Consideration |
| **Use Cases / Case Studies** | 1–2/month | Case study, LinkedIn carousel, email snippet | Consideration → Decision |
| **Webinar** | Monthly | Recap blog, LinkedIn article, email follow-up | Consideration → Decision |
| **CXO Meetup / Events** | Quarterly | Invite sequence, recap, LinkedIn posts | Decision → Pipeline |

---

## Weekly Publishing Rhythm (Template)

| Day | Asset | Pillar | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | TeqTalk LinkedIn post (insight pull) | Podcast | LinkedIn |
| **Tuesday** | Blog or LinkedIn Article | Use Case / Thought Leadership | LinkedIn / Website |
| **Wednesday** | TeqTalk Reel or Quote Card | Podcast | LinkedIn / YouTube Shorts |
| **Thursday** | Case Study or Proof Point Post | Use Case | LinkedIn |
| **Friday** | Community / Engagement Post | Any | LinkedIn |

Adjust based on episode drop schedule and event calendar.

---

## Monthly Calendar Skeleton

### Week 1 — Podcast Launch Week (episode drops)
- Day of drop: Newsletter send + LinkedIn announcement post
- Day 2: LinkedIn insight pull post (from episode)
- Day 3: Reel script published
- Day 4: LinkedIn article (episode theme expanded)
- Day 5: Quote card or carousel

### Week 2 — Use Case / Industry Week
- Blog post published (connects to episode theme or standalone)
- Case study LinkedIn post + link to full
- Email nurture asset (case study snippet for active sequences)
- Carousel: framework or "X signs you have this problem"

### Week 3 — Webinar / Event Prep Week (if applicable)
- Webinar invite sequence activation
- LinkedIn post: topic teaser or speaker spotlight
- Event invite (if CXO meetup/Dreamforce in horizon)

### Week 4 — Proof + Pipeline Week
- Client outcome post (anonymized if needed)
- Battlecard or persona-specific one-pager (for sales team)
- Recap of month's top-performing content (internal — for planning next month)

---

## Content Calendar Build Checklist

When building a calendar for a specific month or quarter, confirm:

- [ ] TeqTalk episode schedule for the period (which episodes drop when)
- [ ] Any events in the period (Dreamforce, webinar, CXO dinner, regional meetup)
- [ ] Active campaigns or verticals to prioritize (e.g., healthcare push in Q3)
- [ ] Case studies or proof points available to publish
- [ ] Any partner co-marketing activity (Salesforce, Snowflake joint content)
- [ ] Team bandwidth — how many net-new assets vs. repurposed pieces

---

## Output Format for a Content Calendar

When producing a calendar, deliver it as a table with these columns:

| Week | Date | Asset Title (draft) | Asset Type | Pillar | Channel | Source Material | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | [date] | [draft headline] | LinkedIn Post | Podcast | LinkedIn | TeqTalk Ep 35 | Draft needed |

Include:
- Draft headline for every asset (not placeholder text like "TBD blog")
- Source material column — what existing content feeds this asset
- Status column: Draft needed / In review / Scheduled / Published

## Seasonal / Event Calendar Triggers

| Trigger | Content Response |
|---|---|
| **Dreamforce** | 6-week pre-event content series + event invite sequence + post-event recap |
| **Salesforce product launch** (Agentforce, Data Cloud update) | 2–3 LinkedIn posts + 1 blog on implications for enterprise |
| **Snowflake Summit** | Co-marketing post + data-focused blog |
| **Healthcare conference season** (HIMSS, ViVE) | Healthcare vertical content push 4 weeks prior |
| **Q4 / Year-end** | "State of [topic]" blog + retrospective LinkedIn article |
| **New TeqTalk guest confirmed** | Pre-episode LinkedIn teaser 1 week before drop |
