# Blog Post Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Blog Role in the Content Mix
Blogs are the SEO and thought leadership backbone of Teqfocus' content engine. Every blog should serve two purposes: (1) rank for a relevant search query, and (2) move a specific buyer persona from awareness to consideration.

Blogs are also the **primary source asset** for LinkedIn articles, email nurture snippets, and carousel repurposing.

---

## Blog Structure (Standard)

### 1. Title
- SEO-friendly, outcome-led, 8–12 words
- Include the primary keyword naturally
- Must answer a real question the target persona is searching for

✅ *"Why Healthcare AI Projects Fail at the Data Layer — And How to Fix It"*
✅ *"Agentforce vs. Traditional Salesforce Automation: What Enterprise Teams Need to Know"*
❌ *"Teqfocus Blog: AI and Healthcare Update"*
❌ *"Leveraging Cutting-Edge AI for Holistic Transformation"*

---

### 2. Opening Hook (1–2 paragraphs)
- Open with the industry problem — not with "In this blog, we will..."
- Make the reader feel seen before offering a solution
- State the central tension or question the blog will resolve

---

### 3. Body (4–6 sections with subheads)
Each section:
- **Subhead** — outcome or insight statement, not a category label
- **2–4 short paragraphs** per section
- Connect to Teqfocus' approach where natural — not forced
- Use specific examples, not generic statements
- Flag unverified stats with `[VERIFY]`

**Subhead formula options:**
- *"The [Problem] Most [Persona] Teams Don't See Until It's Too Late"*
- *"Why [Common Approach] Doesn't Work for [Industry/Use Case]"*
- *"What [Outcome] Actually Requires (It's Not [Common Misconception])"*
- *"The [Architecture / Framework / Approach] That Changes This"*

---

### 4. Teqfocus POV Paragraph (1 paragraph, optional but preferred)
A natural, non-promotional paragraph connecting the blog's central insight to how Teqfocus approaches this problem.

**Rules:**
- Don't announce credentials — let them surface
- Write from experience, not from a feature list
- One paragraph only — not a product pitch

---

### 5. Closing + CTA
- 2–3 lines summarizing the central argument
- Specific CTA — not "contact us to learn more"

✅ *"If you're evaluating your data architecture before an AI rollout, we can walk through where most teams stall — and what they do differently. [Schedule a 30-minute architecture review →]"*
✅ *"Hear how [Guest Name] approached this challenge on TeqTalk Episode [#] → [link]"*
❌ *"Contact us to learn how Teqfocus can help your organization."*

---

## Blog Length Guide

| Type | Word Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Cornerstone / Pillar Post** | 1,500–2,500 words | SEO anchor, comprehensive coverage of a topic |
| **Standard Blog** | 800–1,200 words | Awareness + consideration, most common format |
| **Short Take / POV Post** | 400–600 words | Quick perspective, trend response, LinkedIn article seed |

---

## SEO Principles for Teqfocus Blogs

- **Target one primary keyword per post** — don't try to rank for 5 things
- **Use the keyword in:** title, first paragraph, 1–2 subheads, meta description
- **Internal link** to relevant case studies or other blogs where natural
- **External link** to credible sources for any stat or claim `[VERIFY before publishing]`
- **Image alt text** should describe the image and include the keyword where natural

---

## Blog → Repurposing Checklist

After every blog is published, flag these for the distribution layer:

- [ ] LinkedIn post (contrarian take or insight pull from the blog)
- [ ] LinkedIn article (adapted version with article-specific intro)
- [ ] LinkedIn carousel (if the blog contains a framework or list)
- [ ] Email nurture snippet (80–100 word version for mid-sequence)
- [ ] TeqTalk tie-in (if the blog topic matches a recent episode)

---

## Banned Phrases in Teqfocus Blogs
- "In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape..."
- "Leverage synergies across..."
- "Holistic, end-to-end solution..."
- "Best-in-class, cutting-edge..."
- "We are proud to offer..."
- "Our robust platform..."
- Any sentence that starts with "Teqfocus is excited to..."
