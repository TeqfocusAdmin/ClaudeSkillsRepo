# Reference: Short-Form Video Script

Applies to: LinkedIn video · Instagram Reels · YouTube Shorts · TikTok
Length: 30 seconds = ~75 words | 60 seconds = ~150 words | 90 seconds = ~225 words

## Structure

### Hook (first 3 seconds — non-negotiable)
The viewer decides to watch or scroll in the first 3 seconds.
Options:
- Bold contrarian claim: "Most Salesforce implementations are set up to fail."
- Direct question: "Why does your AI project keep stalling at the data layer?"
- Surprising stat: "73% of enterprise AI projects never reach production."
- Pattern interrupt: "[Pause] Nobody talks about this in enterprise AI."

### Problem (5–10 seconds)
- Name the pain in 1–2 sentences
- Make it feel personal — "you're probably doing X"
- No solution yet

### Insight / Solution (10–20 seconds)
- Core idea — the thing most people miss
- 1 concrete example or analogy
- Keep it simple — one idea per video

### Proof (5–10 seconds, optional)
- 1 client outcome or verified stat
- Brief — "We saw this cut [metric] by [X] at a [industry] company"

### CTA (final 5 seconds)
- Single action: "Follow for more" / "Link in bio to book a call" / "Comment [X] and I'll send you..."
- Conversational — not "click here"

## Format Per Platform

| Platform | Length | Caption | Captions on video |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn video | 30–90 sec | 150–300 chars + hashtags | Recommended |
| Instagram Reels | 15–60 sec | Short caption, 3–5 hashtags | Required |
| YouTube Shorts | 15–60 sec | Title ≤60 chars | Recommended |

## Script Format to Deliver

```
VIDEO SCRIPT: [Title]
Platform: [LinkedIn / Reels / Shorts]
Length: [seconds]
Speaker: [Avi / Andy / [Name]]

---
[0:00–0:03] HOOK
[Visual: talking head / B-roll suggestion]
Script: "[Exact words to say]"

[0:03–0:12] PROBLEM
Script: "..."

[0:12–0:35] INSIGHT
Script: "..."

[0:35–0:45] PROOF (optional)
Script: "..."

[0:45–0:55] CTA
Script: "..."
---

CAPTION:
[Full caption copy including hashtags]

ON-SCREEN TEXT (if any):
- [Timestamp]: "[Text to overlay]"
```

## Voice Rules
- Conversational — write how the speaker actually talks
- No marketing speak — this is a person sharing an insight, not a brand ad
- First person for personal profiles (Avi, Andy)
- Branded/third person for company channel
- American English throughout
