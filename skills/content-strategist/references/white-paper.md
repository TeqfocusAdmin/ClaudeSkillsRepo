# Reference: White Paper / Thought Leadership Report

Length: 2,000–4,000 words | Format: .docx or .pdf via teqfocus-design skill
Gated asset — used for lead generation and authority building.

## Structure

### 1. Cover Page
- Title: outcome-led, specific — not "AI in Healthcare" but "Why 73% of Healthcare AI Projects Fail Before Reaching Production"
- Sub-title: 1 sentence framing the reader's problem
- Author: Teqfocus practice lead name + title
- Date: Month Year

### 2. Executive Summary (200–300 words)
- The problem in 2 sentences
- Why existing approaches fall short in 2 sentences
- Teqfocus's perspective in 3 sentences
- What the reader will learn — 3 bullets

### 3. The Problem Section (400–600 words)
- Industry data point to open (cited, verified — flag with [VERIFY] if uncertain)
- Root cause analysis — 3 layers deep, not surface symptoms
- Real-world consequence for the buyer persona
- Transition: "The challenge isn't [obvious thing] — it's [non-obvious root cause]"

### 4. Why Current Approaches Fail (300–500 words)
- 3 common approaches and why each falls short
- No competitor names — frame by approach type
- Each failure connected to a business cost or missed outcome

### 5. A Better Framework (600–900 words)
- Teqfocus's approach — named framework or methodology
- 3–5 principles or steps, each with:
  - What it means
  - Why it matters
  - What it looks like in practice
- One brief client example per principle (anonymized if needed)

### 6. Case Study / Proof Section (300–500 words)
- Challenge → Solution → Business Outcomes format
- Verified metrics only — flag [VERIFY] if uncertain
- Industry-matched to target reader

### 7. Conclusion + CTA (200–300 words)
- Restate the core insight
- 3 action steps the reader can take now
- Single CTA: "Book a [specific session type] with our team"

## Voice Rules
- Practitioner voice — not marketing copy
- Third person for Teqfocus references in body; first person acceptable in conclusion
- Every claim supported by data or client example
- No jargon without definition
- American English throughout
