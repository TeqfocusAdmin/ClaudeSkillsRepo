# Reference: GTM Newsletter

The general Teqfocus GTM newsletter — sent to prospects, partners, and the
broader network. Distinct from TeqTalk episode newsletters and demand-gen sequences.

Length: 300–500 words | Frequency: bi-weekly or monthly | 3 sections

## Format

```
SUBJECT: [Under 50 characters — specific insight, not "Monthly Update"]
PREVIEW TEXT: [Extends subject — 80–100 characters]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[MONTH YEAR] | Teqfocus GTM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 1 — THE INSIGHT
[One market observation, trend signal, or pattern Teqfocus is seeing
across client conversations. 80–100 words. Practitioner voice —
not a market research summary. Specific, opinionated, useful.]

SECTION 2 — FROM THE FIELD
[One brief proof point or outcome — a client win, a delivery milestone,
a notable engagement. 60–80 words. Industry-matched to the audience.
No invented metrics — flag with [VERIFY] if uncertain.
Natural mention of Teqfocus's role — never announce Summit credential here.]

SECTION 3 — ONE THING WORTH YOUR TIME
[A single recommendation — a resource, a framework, a question to ask
your team. 40–60 words. Genuinely useful — not self-promotional.
CTA: one specific, frictionless action.
Example: "If your data readiness is blocking your AI roadmap —
[book a 30-minute architecture review →]"]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[Warm sign-off — from a named person, not "The Teqfocus Team"]
[Unsubscribe]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## Subject Line Formulas
- "[Insight in 6–8 words]" — e.g., "Why your AI stalls at the data layer"
- "The question every [persona] is asking right now"
- "[Contrarian take in ≤8 words]"
- Never: "Monthly Newsletter", "Updates from Teqfocus", "Check out what's new"

## Voice Rules
- Practitioner to practitioner — not brand to customer
- Section 1: opinionated — share a point of view, not a summary
- Section 2: specific — one client situation, briefly told
- Section 3: genuinely useful — something the reader can act on today
- American English throughout
