# Sales Deck Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## Standard Deck Structure (10–14 slides)

| Slide | Purpose | Writing Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cover** | Company + bold outcome statement | Never open with "About Teqfocus." Open with a problem or outcome the buyer recognizes. |
| **2. The Industry Problem** | Name the specific pain | Buyer's language only. Make them think: "That's exactly where we are." |
| **3. Why This Is Harder Now** | Urgency + stakes | Tie to AI mandate, data complexity, competitive pressure, or regulatory shift. |
| **4. Why Fragmented Solutions Fail** | Competitive wedge | Position against single-layer partners (CRM-only, data-only) without naming them. |
| **5. The Teqfocus Approach** | Our method | Problem → Industry-specific design → Multi-cloud architecture → Measured outcome. |
| **6. The Full-Stack Advantage** | Architecture story | Show how Data + AI + Application layers connect. `[DESIGN FLAG: architecture diagram]` |
| **7. What We Actually Build** | Service specifics | Translate platform features into operational outcomes. No product feature slides. |
| **8. Industry Proof** | Case study or proof point | One strong story. Challenge → What we did → The number. Always needs a metric. |
| **9. Why Teqfocus** | Differentiators | Summit Partner + full-stack depth + industry IP + speed-to-value. |
| **10. Engagement Model** | How we work | De-risk the decision. Phases, milestones, what they own vs. we own. |
| **11. Next Step** | Specific CTA | Not "let's talk." Offer a defined workshop, assessment, or scoped first call. |

*Add as needed: Team bios, tech stack diagram, certification logos, partner badges.*

---

## Deck Writing Rules

- Every **slide headline = a complete outcome or insight**, never a category label
  - ✅ *"Healthcare CIOs Reduce Integration Costs With a Unified Data + Salesforce Architecture"*
  - ❌ *"Our Healthcare Capabilities"*
- Bullets = **outcome + evidence**, not feature lists
- Assume the deck **may be read without you in the room** — every slide must stand alone
- Slide 8 (proof) must include at least one metric — use `[ADD METRIC]` if unavailable
- Language: executive reading level — not technical jargon, not entry-level
- Banned words: "leverage," "synergy," "holistic," "best-in-class," "robust," "end-to-end"

## Persona-Specific Deck Angles

### For CIO / CTO
- Slide 2: Lead with integration complexity and technical debt from failed transformations
- Slide 4: "Vendors who only solve one layer leave you coordinating the rest"
- Slide 6: Emphasize architecture stability, phased delivery, minimal disruption
- Slide 9: Summit credential as risk reduction, not just a badge

### For CDO / Head of Data & AI
- Slide 2: Lead with data readiness gap — "AI fails when the data layer isn't ready"
- Slide 4: "Pure-play data firms can't connect it to your applications. Salesforce-only SIs ignore the data layer."
- Slide 6: Snowflake + Databricks + Data Cloud as the unified foundation
- Slide 9: Governed, AI-ready data architecture as the differentiator

### For RevOps / Mid-Market Ops
- Slide 2: Lead with CRM adoption failure and forecast inaccuracy
- Slide 3: "Your Salesforce investment is underperforming — not because of the platform, but the implementation"
- Keep slides 5–6 lighter on architecture; focus on workflow and adoption
- Slide 11 CTA: Offer a 30-day Salesforce health check or optimization sprint

## Slide Headline Formula

Use this pattern when writing slide headlines:

**[Audience] + [Verb/Outcome] + [With/By/Through] + [Approach or Credential]**

Examples:
- *"Enterprise Healthcare Teams Cut Care Coordination Costs With AI-Powered Interoperability"*
- *"Mid-Market Sales Teams Close Faster When Salesforce Is Built the Right Way"*
- *"CDOs Accelerate AI Adoption When Data Infrastructure Comes First"*

## Proof Slide (Slide 8) Formula

Structure every proof slide as:

**Client Context** (1 line — industry, size, challenge type. No client name if not approved.)
**The Problem** (1–2 lines — specific, not generic)
**What Teqfocus Did** (2–3 bullets — specific to the stack and approach)
**The Outcome** (1–2 metrics in a callout box) `[DESIGN FLAG: metric callout]`

If no approved case study is available: Use `[ADD APPROVED CASE STUDY: industry]` as a placeholder — do not fabricate.
