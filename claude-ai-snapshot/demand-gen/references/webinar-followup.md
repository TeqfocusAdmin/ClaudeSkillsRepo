# Webinar Follow-Up Sequence Reference — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
After any Teqfocus-hosted or co-hosted webinar. Applies to attendees (showed up), registered no-shows (signed up but didn't attend), and replay requesters.

**Three separate tracks — do not mix them.**

---

## Track 1: Attendees (Showed Up)

**Cadence:** Email 1 within 24 hours → Email 2 on Day 4 → Email 3 on Day 9
**Tone:** Peer-level. They gave you an hour — acknowledge it and give them more value.

### Email 1 — The Thank You + Instant Value (Send within 24 hours)

**Goal:** Reinforce the value of attending. Deliver on any promised follow-up resources.

```
Subject: [Webinar title] — recording + key takeaways
Preview: Plus the one thing we didn't have time to cover

[Name], thanks for joining [webinar title] yesterday.

Here's what we promised:
→ Recording: [link]
→ [Any slide deck / resource promised]: [link]

The question that came up most in the chat — [most common 
question from the session] — is worth a proper answer. 
Here's [a resource / our take in 2 lines].

If you're actively working through [topic], I'm happy to 
set aside 30 minutes for a more specific conversation about 
your situation.

[Calendar link]

[First name]
```

### Email 2 — The Depth Add (Day 4)

**Goal:** Extend the webinar's core theme. Give them one thing they can use this week.

- Pull the sharpest insight from the webinar
- Add one layer of depth not covered in the session
- Link to a relevant case study or blog
- CTA: Soft — "Does this match what you're seeing?"

### Email 3 — The Meeting Ask (Day 9)

**Goal:** Convert to a meeting. Direct but specific.

- Reference the webinar topic and connect it to their likely situation
- Frame a specific session type — not a generic demo
- CTA: Calendar link with specific session name

```
Subject: [Specific session offer based on webinar topic]
Preview: [What they'd walk away with]

[Name], given your interest in [webinar topic], I think the 
most useful next step would be a [session name] — a 30-minute 
conversation where we look at [specific thing they'd review 
or assess].

No deck. Just a focused conversation about where [their 
industry/role] teams are hitting friction and what the path 
forward looks like.

[Calendar link]

[First name]
```

## Track 2: Registered No-Shows

**Cadence:** Email 1 within 24 hours → Email 2 on Day 5 → Email 3 on Day 10
**Tone:** No guilt. They got busy — give them the value anyway.

### Email 1 — The Recording Send (Within 24 hours)

```
Subject: You registered for [webinar] — here's the recording
Preview: Plus the 3 minutes worth watching most

[Name], we missed you at [webinar title] — totally understand 
things come up.

Here's the recording: [link]

If you only have 10 minutes: [timestamp range] covers 
[the most valuable section]. That's the part most attendees 
said they'll use immediately.

[First name]
```

### Email 2 — The Key Takeaway (Day 5)

- Don't assume they watched the recording
- Pull the single most actionable insight from the webinar
- Write it so it stands alone — they get value without watching
- CTA: Resource or soft question

### Email 3 — The Offer (Day 10)

- Same structure as Track 1 Email 3
- Slightly softer framing — they haven't engaged yet

---

## Track 3: Replay Requesters

**Cadence:** Email 1 when replay is sent → Email 2 on Day 5 → Email 3 on Day 10

### Email 1 — The Replay Delivery

```
Subject: Your replay: [webinar title]
Preview: [Most-watched moment + timestamp]

[Name], here's the replay of [webinar title]: [link]

The section most people jump to: [timestamp] — [2-line 
description of why that part is worth it].

If you have questions after watching, happy to continue 
the conversation.

[First name]
```

Emails 2 and 3 follow Track 2 structure.

---

## Webinar Follow-Up Rules

- **Email 1 always within 24 hours** — the conversation is most alive immediately after
- **Always deliver the recording** — even if they attended, make it easy to share internally
- **Never guilt no-shows** — they're still warm leads
- **Timestamp the recording** — "jump to [MM:SS] for [topic]" dramatically increases replay engagement
- **Track separately in HubSpot** — attendees, no-shows, and replay requesters need different lead scores
- **Subject lines for follow-ups:** name the webinar topic, not just "webinar follow-up"
