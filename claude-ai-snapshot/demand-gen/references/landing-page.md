# Landing Page & Lead Magnet Copy Reference — Teqfocus Demand Gen

## When to Use
Writing copy for: webinar registration pages, gated resource download pages (guides, playbooks, assessment tools), event registration pages, and any page where a prospect exchanges contact details for Teqfocus content.

---

## Landing Page Copy Structure

### 1. Headline
**Job:** Make the value of the resource / event unmistakably clear in one line.
**Formula:** [Outcome or insight] + [For whom] + [In what context]

✅ *"How Healthcare CIOs Are Building AI-Ready Data Infrastructure — Without Starting Over"*
✅ *"The Salesforce Architecture Mistakes Mid-Market Teams Make in Year 2 (And How to Fix Them)"*
❌ *"Teqfocus Webinar: AI and Digital Transformation"*
❌ *"Download Our Guide to Data Strategy"*

---

### 2. Subheadline (1–2 lines)
Expands the headline with specificity. Names the problem being solved or the specific insight being delivered.

> *"Most AI initiatives stall at the data layer — not the model layer. This session maps the 4 architecture decisions that determine whether your AI investment delivers or stalls."*

---

### 3. Bullet Points (3–5 bullets)
What they will learn / get / walk away with. Outcome-framed — not topic-listed.

✅ *"Why interoperability is a governance problem, not a technical one — and how to solve it in your org"*
✅ *"The data architecture pattern that's working across [industry] AI deployments right now"*
❌ *"Overview of data strategy considerations"*
❌ *"Discussion of Salesforce and AI"*

---

### 4. Social Proof Block (1–2 proof points)
- Teqfocus Summit Partnership credential (natural, not announced)
- Speaker credentials for webinars
- Industry-specific client outcome if available `[VERIFY before publishing]`
- TeqTalk episode count / guest caliber if relevant

---

### 5. Form
**Fields:** First name, last name, work email, company, role/title
**Optional:** Industry, team size (only add if it's used for segmentation — every extra field reduces conversion)
**CTA Button Text:**

✅ *"Reserve My Spot"* (events)
✅ *"Get the Guide"* (downloads)
✅ *"Watch the Replay"* (recordings)
❌ *"Submit"*
❌ *"Click Here"*
❌ *"Download Now"* (too generic)

---

### 6. Below-the-Fold: About the Host / Speaker
- 3–4 lines on speaker credentials or Teqfocus context
- Written in third person, outcome-focused
- No corporate biography language

---

## Lead Magnet Types & Copy Approach

### Webinar Registration Page
- Headline: What they'll learn + who the speaker is
- Bullets: 3–4 specific takeaways
- Speaker bio: Short, credibility-focused
- Logistics: Date, time, timezone — always visible above the fold
- CTA: "Reserve My Spot" or "Register Free"

### Guide / Playbook Download
- Headline: The problem the guide solves
- Bullets: What's inside — named sections or frameworks, not vague "comprehensive coverage"
- Trust signal: Teqfocus credential + any client context
- CTA: "Get the [Guide Name]"

### Assessment / Diagnostic Tool
- Headline: What they'll discover about their situation
- Bullets: What the assessment covers + what they'll get at the end
- CTA: "Start the Assessment" or "See Where You Stand"

### Event Registration (Executive Dinner / Meetup)
- Headline: The event name + city + date — immediately visible
- Body: Format description (dinner, roundtable, etc.) + topic + who it's for
- Exclusivity signal where appropriate: "Limited to [X] attendees" or "Invitation-only"
- CTA: "Request My Seat" or "Confirm Attendance"

---

## Landing Page Copy Rules

- **Above the fold must answer:** What is this, who is it for, and what will I get?
- **Never bury the topic** — the headline must name the specific subject, not tease it
- **Bullets are outcomes, not features** — "You'll walk away knowing X" not "We'll cover X"
- **Social proof is supporting evidence** — it reinforces the decision, it doesn't lead
- **Form friction kills conversion** — only ask for fields you will actually use
- **Every landing page needs a thank-you page** — with the resource / confirmation + a next step CTA

---

## Post-Download / Post-Registration Thank You Page

Always include:
1. Confirmation: "You're registered" / "Your guide is on its way"
2. Immediate value: Relevant case study, TeqTalk episode, or blog link
3. Next step CTA: "Want to talk through how this applies to your situation?" + calendar link
4. Social share prompt (optional): LinkedIn share link for the webinar/resource
