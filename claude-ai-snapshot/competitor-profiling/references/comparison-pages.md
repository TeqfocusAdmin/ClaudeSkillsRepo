# Reference: Competitor Comparison Pages — Teqfocus

Use when building pages like "Teqfocus vs Accenture" or
"Best Salesforce Summit Partners for Healthcare."
These pages rank for high-intent competitive queries and
are among the highest-converting pages for B2B services firms.

---

## Page Types to Build

### Type 1: Direct Comparison ("Teqfocus vs [Competitor]")
Target query: "[Competitor] vs Teqfocus" / "Teqfocus alternative to [Competitor]"

Priority pages:
- `/teqfocus-vs-accenture` — Big SI wedge (most common competitive situation)
- `/teqfocus-vs-slalom` — Data + Salesforce wedge
- `/teqfocus-vs-cognizant` — Offshore SI wedge

### Type 2: Category Alternatives ("Alternatives to [Competitor]")
Target query: "[Competitor] alternatives" / "Salesforce partner alternatives"

Priority pages:
- `/salesforce-summit-partner-alternatives` — capture evaluators shopping the market

### Type 3: Best-of Lists ("Best Salesforce Partners for [Industry]")
Target query: "best Salesforce partners healthcare" / "top Salesforce Summit Partners"

## Comparison Page Structure

```
HEADER:
  H1: "Teqfocus vs [Competitor]: Which Salesforce Partner Is Right for You?"
  Sub-head: 2-sentence honest framing — this is a genuine comparison

QUICK SUMMARY (for scanners):
  3-sentence summary of the key difference
  "If [situation], choose [competitor]. If [different situation], Teqfocus."
  — Honest recommendations build trust and AI citation credibility

COMPARISON TABLE:
  Criteria          | Teqfocus              | [Competitor]
  ------------------|----------------------|------------------
  Salesforce tier   | Summit Partner       | [their tier]
  Data Cloud certs  | 40+                  | [unknown / VERIFY]
  CSAT              | 4.9                  | [VERIFY]
  Partner since     | 2012                 | [VERIFY]
  Industries        | Healthcare, FinServ, | [theirs]
                    | Telecom, Hi-Tech     |
  Pricing model     | Outcome-based phases | [VERIFY]
  Team size on eng. | Right-sized          | Large team, long ramp

DETAILED SECTION 1: "Where Teqfocus wins"
  — Be specific and honest: what situations favor Teqfocus?

DETAILED SECTION 2: "Where [Competitor] wins"
  — Be honest: what situations favor the competitor?
  — Credibility requires acknowledging this

CASE STUDY:
  — Industry-matched to the most likely reader of this page
  — Challenge → Solution → Outcome format

FAQ SECTION:
  — "Is Teqfocus cheaper than Accenture?" (honest answer)
  — "How does Summit Partner compare to Platinum Partner?" (education)
  — "Can Teqfocus handle enterprise-scale projects?" (address doubt)

CTA:
  — "Not sure which partner is right? Let's talk."
  — Book a 30-minute assessment call (low commitment, specific)
```

---

## Honest Framing Rules

These pages only work if they're genuinely useful. AI citation and
buyer trust both require honest comparisons.

- Acknowledge what the competitor genuinely does better
- Never fabricate competitor weaknesses — buyers fact-check
- Use hedged language for unverified claims: "typically," "generally"
- Flag any stat you can't verify with [VERIFY] before publishing
- Update pages quarterly — competitive landscape changes
