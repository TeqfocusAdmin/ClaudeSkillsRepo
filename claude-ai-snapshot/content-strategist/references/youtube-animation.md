# YouTube Animation Brief Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Purpose
YouTube animation briefs guide the video editor or motion graphics team on what to overlay on top of a TeqTalk episode recording. The goal is to create an engaging long-form YouTube video that makes the conversation visually dynamic — without distracting from the speakers.

---

## Animation Style Philosophy
**Inspired by:** WTF with Nikhil Kamath — conversational podcast + minimal, purposeful overlays
**Not:** Full-screen animations, kinetic typography walls, or heavy motion graphics

The animation should feel like it's *helping* the viewer track the conversation — highlighting what matters, visualizing what's abstract — not performing on top of it.

---

## Brief Structure (Deliver for every episode)

### 1. Episode Context (4–6 lines)
- Guest name, title, organization
- Episode number and title
- Core topic and 3 key themes covered
- Audience this episode is for (persona)

---

### 2. Animation Style Spec

**Overlay Type:** Minimal icon-based motion graphics + keyword highlights
**Placement:** Lower third and side panel — NOT full screen
**Font:** Clean sans-serif, consistent with Teqfocus brand
**Color:** Teqfocus brand palette — `[DESIGN FLAG: Navy #59267E · Red #7751E2 · Dark #244495 · White #FFFFFF · Gray #8E9BF5]`
**Logo:** TeqTalk logo bug — lower right corner, persistent throughout

**What gets animated:**
- Key stats or numbers mentioned by the guest
- Defined terms or concepts the guest introduces
- Named frameworks, models, or methodologies
- Architecture diagrams (simple, schematic-style)
- Guest name + title (lower third on intro and after any cut)

**What does NOT get animated:**
- General conversation — let it breathe
- Emotional or storytelling moments — don't overlay these
- Transitions between topics — hold on speakers

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Over-animating** — If everything is highlighted, nothing is
- **Animating filler** — Only animate moments that add comprehension or emphasis
- **Off-brand fonts** — Always use approved Teqfocus typography
- **Missing lower thirds** — Guest must always be identified on screen
- **Slow animations** — Motion should feel crisp, not sluggish. In and out in under 0.5 sec for most elements
