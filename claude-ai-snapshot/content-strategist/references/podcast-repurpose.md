# Podcast Repurposing Reference — Teqfocus Content Strategist

## Core Rule
Never summarize the whole episode in a single asset. Extract one sharp insight and let it stand alone. The episode link is always the destination — the repurposed asset is the reason to go there.

## Transcript Trimming Protocol

When given a raw transcript with timestamps:

1. **Read the full transcript first** — identify the 5–7 most quotable or insight-dense moments
2. **Mark sections for trimming** — sections that are filler, repetitive, or off-topic
   - In the output, **bold the sections marked for trim** — do not edit the text, bold only
   - Preserve all timestamps exactly as they appear
3. **Flag key moments** with a comment: `[KEY MOMENT: reason]`
4. **Never rewrite** transcript content — only trim and flag

**Trim criteria:**
- Extended pleasantries or small talk at open/close
- Repeated points the guest makes more than once
- Technical tangents not relevant to the episode's core theme
- Long pauses, filler words clusters ("um", "you know", "like" — flag if excessive)

## Short-Form Reel Script (45–90 seconds)

### Structure

**Hook (0–5 sec)**
Bold on-screen text + spoken claim. Must stop the scroll.
- The sharpest, most debatable, or most surprising thing from the episode
- Must work as on-screen text AND audio hook simultaneously
`[DESIGN FLAG: keyword highlight animation on key phrase]`

**Setup (5–20 sec)**
Brief context. Who is saying this and why their perspective matters.

**The Insight (20–60 sec)**
The guest's key point — delivered directly, not narrated
- Write in spoken language, not written/formal
- One idea only — never try to cover the episode

**The Land (60–80 sec)**
One line that gives the viewer something to do with this insight.
- A reframe, a question, or an action

**CTA (80–90 sec)**
> *"Full episode on TeqTalk → link in bio"*
`[DESIGN FLAG: TeqTalk logo bug — lower right, persistent]`
`[DESIGN FLAG: guest name lower third]`

### Reel Writing Rules
- One insight per reel — never multiple
- Spoken language only — contractions, short sentences, natural rhythm
- Never use corporate language in reels — if it sounds like a press release, rewrite it
- Hook must create a pattern interrupt — assume the viewer is mid-scroll

## LinkedIn Post from Podcast (Insight Pull)

**Structure:**
- **Hook** = sharpest insight or most counterintuitive claim from the guest
- **Body (3–4 lines)** = expand or react to the insight — add context, tension, or implications
- **Attribution** = *"— [Guest Name], [Title], TeqTalk Episode [#]"*
- **CTA** = link to full episode

**Rule:** The post must stand alone as an insight — not as a promotion. The episode link is the bonus, not the point.

---

## LinkedIn Article from Podcast

Build a full thought leadership piece around the episode's **central theme** — not the episode itself.

**Structure:**
1. Thumbnail title — SEO-friendly, outcome-led
2. Challenge intro — Open with the industry problem the episode addresses
3. Core insights (3–5 subheads) — Guest quotes used as supporting evidence, not the main story
4. Teqfocus perspective — 1 paragraph connecting the theme to how Teqfocus approaches it
5. CTA → *"Hear the full conversation: [link]"*

**Length:** 600–900 words

## YouTube Animation Brief

For each TeqTalk episode uploaded to YouTube:

**Brief Structure:**
1. **Episode Context** — Guest name, title, core topic, key themes (3–4 lines)
2. **Animation Style** — Icon-based motion graphics, keyword highlights, minimal overlay (not full-screen). Inspired by conversational podcast visual style (WTF Kamath-style overlays).
3. **Segment Breakdown Table:**

| Timestamp | On-Screen Visual | Keyword / Stat to Highlight |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–02:30 | Guest intro lower third | Guest name + title |
| [timestamp] | [icon or animation type] | [keyword or number] |

4. **Branding Notes** — White `#FFFFFF` background always. Headline: `#59267E`. Accent/lower thirds bar: `#7751E2`. Body text: `#244495`. Logo top-left or bottom-right.
5. **Key Moments to Animate** — The 3–5 most quotable or data-heavy moments in the episode
