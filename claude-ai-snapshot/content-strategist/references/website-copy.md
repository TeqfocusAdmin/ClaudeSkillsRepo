# Reference: Website Copy

Applies to: Service pages · Homepage sections · Industry vertical pages ·
Landing pages · About page

## Page Structure by Type

### Service Page (e.g., "Salesforce Transformation" or "AI & Data")

**Hero section:**
- Headline: ≤10 words — outcome-led, specific
  ✅ "Salesforce That Performs — Not Just Goes Live"
  ❌ "Comprehensive Salesforce Implementation Services"
- Sub-headline: 1 sentence — who this is for + what they get
- CTA: single button — "Book a Discovery Call" or "See Our Work"

**Problem section (50–80 words):**
- 2–3 sentences naming the pain the buyer knows
- No solution mention yet — validate the problem first

**Solution section (100–150 words):**
- What Teqfocus does, specifically
- 3 capability bullets — each outcome-connected, not feature-listed
  ✅ "Data architecture that gives Agentforce something real to work with"
  ❌ "We design and implement data warehousing solutions"

**Proof section (50–100 words):**
- 1 verified stat or client outcome
- 1-line case study reference (link to full case study)

**How it works (3 steps max):**
- Step name + 1-sentence description
- Outcome of each step — not just activity

**CTA section:**
- Headline: action-oriented
- 1 paragraph supporting
- Single button

### Homepage Hero

- Headline: core positioning in 8–12 words
  "The partner that connects your data, AI, and Salesforce — and delivers results"
- Sub-headline: 1–2 sentences expanding on the headline
- 2 CTAs max: primary (book call) + secondary (see work/case studies)

### Industry Vertical Page

Same structure as service page but:
- Hero headline uses industry-specific language (payer, not "healthcare company")
- Problem section references industry-specific challenges (prior auth, churn, AUM)
- Proof section uses industry-matched case study
- Solution section references relevant Salesforce clouds (Health Cloud, FinServ Cloud)

## SEO Considerations
- Primary keyword in H1 and first 100 words
- Secondary keywords in H2 headings
- Meta title: ≤60 characters | Meta description: ≤155 characters
- Internal links to related service/case study pages

## Voice Rules
- Active voice — "We build" not "Solutions are built"
- Second person — "your data", "your team", "your Salesforce"
- No jargon without immediate context
- American English throughout
