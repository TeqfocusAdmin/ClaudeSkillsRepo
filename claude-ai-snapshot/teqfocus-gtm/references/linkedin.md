# LinkedIn Content Reference — Teqfocus GTM Skill

## Content Types

### 1. LinkedIn Posts (Organic Feed)

**Structure:**
- **Line 1 (Hook):** Bold claim, counterintuitive statement, or sharp question. Never start with "I" or "We." No emojis in the hook.
- **Lines 2–5 (Body):** 3–5 insight lines. One idea per line. White space between each.
- **Line 6 (Bridge):** Connect the insight back to Teqfocus' POV or a relevant proof point
- **CTA (Final line):** Specific. Drop the link, tag the guest, name the resource.

**Length:** 800–1,300 characters for organic reach
**Tone:** Practitioner authority — not marketing copy. Think: the smartest person in the room, not the loudest.
**Banned openers:** "Excited to share...", "Thrilled to announce...", "We are proud to...", "I", "We"

**Post formats that work well for Teqfocus:**
- **The Contrarian Take:** "Everyone says [X]. Here's why that's wrong in enterprise AI."
- **The Insight From the Field:** "A CDO told us last week: [specific, real-sounding observation]. Here's what that actually means."
- **The Framework:** "3 reasons AI projects stall at the data layer — and what actually fixes them."
- **The Industry Spotlight:** "Healthcare CIOs aren't asking 'should we adopt AI?' anymore. They're asking [new, more interesting question]."
- **The Proof Point:** "[Outcome achieved]. Here's the architecture that made it possible."

---

### 2. LinkedIn Articles

**Structure:**
1. **Thumbnail Title** — SEO-friendly, outcome-led, 8–12 words
2. **Challenge Intro** (2–3 paragraphs) — Open with the industry problem. Make the reader feel seen before you offer anything.
3. **Core Value Section** (3–5 subheads with 2–3 paragraphs each) — One insight or use case per subhead. Connect to Teqfocus' stack where natural — not forced.
4. **Hook to Full Resource** — Link to the related blog, case study, or webinar
5. **Caption + Hashtags** — 3–5 relevant hashtags. No more.

**Length:** 600–900 words
**Tone:** Thought leadership — bold POV, backed by experience or data

---

### 3. LinkedIn Carousels (Document Posts)

**Slide structure:**
- **Slide 1:** Bold claim or sharp question. Big type. `[DESIGN FLAG: high-contrast cover]`
- **Slides 2–7:** One insight per slide. Short headline + 1–2 supporting lines.
- **Slide 8 (Last):** CTA + Teqfocus logo + website

**Content that works as carousels:**
- "5 Signs Your Salesforce Implementation Is Holding You Back"
- "The Data Layer Every AI Project Needs (And Most Companies Are Missing)"
- "How [Industry] Leaders Are Using Agentforce in 2025"

## LinkedIn Voice Rules

**Always:**
- Write from a practitioner's POV — not a marketing department's
- Use specific industry language the target persona uses themselves
- Connect capability to outcome — never feature-list
- Let Teqfocus' credentials surface naturally (not announced)

**Never:**
- Open with "We're excited to..." or "Proud to share..."
- Use vague superlatives: "cutting-edge," "best-in-class," "innovative solutions"
- Stack platform names without context: "Using Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, and Tableau..."
- Announce Summit Partnership as if it's news

## Post Templates by Persona Target

### Targeting CDOs / Data Leaders
Hook formula: *"[Specific AI failure mode] isn't a model problem. It's a data problem."*
Body: Name the data layer gap → explain why it kills AI adoption → frame Teqfocus' architecture approach

### Targeting CIOs / CTOs
Hook formula: *"The most expensive Salesforce mistake isn't a bad license deal."*
Body: Name the hidden cost (bad architecture, low adoption, integration debt) → pivot to what good looks like

### Targeting RevOps / VP Sales
Hook formula: *"Your Salesforce forecast is probably wrong. Here's why."*
Body: Common CRM failure modes → what a well-built Salesforce actually looks like → Teqfocus proof point

## TeqTalk Episode Repurposing on LinkedIn

When repurposing a TeqTalk episode into LinkedIn content:

**Post format:**
- Hook = sharpest insight or most counterintuitive claim from the guest
- 3–4 lines expanding on the insight
- Attribution: *"[Guest Name], [Title], on TeqTalk Episode [#]"*
- CTA: Link to episode

**Article format:**
- Build a full thought leadership piece around the episode's central theme
- Guest quotes used as supporting evidence — not the main story
- CTA: *"Hear the full conversation → [link]"*

**Carousel format:**
- "5 Things [Guest Name] Said About [Topic] That Every [Persona] Should Hear"
- Each slide = one quote or insight, paraphrased tightly
