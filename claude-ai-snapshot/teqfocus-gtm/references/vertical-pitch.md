# Reference: Industry Vertical Pitch

Tailored pitch narrative for a specific industry vertical.
Use when the sales deck needs to be deeply industry-specific, not generic.
Covers: Healthcare · Financial Services · Telecom · Retail/CPG · Hi-Tech/SaaS

## Structure (adapt to deck or document format)

### Opening: The Industry Problem Right Now
- 1 data point specific to this vertical (cited, verified — [VERIFY] if uncertain)
- The tension: technology promise vs operational reality
- Why this is worse / more urgent right now than 2 years ago

### Healthcare
- Core pain: Fragmented data preventing AI-ready care coordination
- Buyer language: prior auth, care gaps, FHIR, interoperability, payer-provider friction
- Key platforms: Health Cloud, Data Cloud, Agentforce, MuleSoft
- Lead proof: patient engagement, connected care, home health, specialty pharmacy
- Key stat for opening: "94% of healthcare providers say prior authorization delays impact patient care [AMA]"
- Differentiator: "We don't just implement Health Cloud — we connect it to the data infrastructure that makes AI clinically actionable"

### Financial Services
- Core pain: Siloed client data preventing personalization at scale; compliance overhead
- Buyer language: AUM, client 360, Know Your Customer, claims processing, underwriting
- Key platforms: Financial Services Cloud, Data Cloud, MuleSoft (BIAN/ACORD connectors)
- Lead proof: policy management, client onboarding, claims automation
- Differentiator: "One view of the client — from advisor to claims to digital banking"

### Telecom
- Core pain: Churn, fragmented customer records, AI stalled on legacy infrastructure
- Buyer language: ARPU, churn rate, next-best-action, BSS/OSS, IoT data
- Key platforms: Data Cloud, Salesforce Service Cloud, Databricks, Snowflake
- Lead proof: churn intervention, network ops analytics, customer 360
- Differentiator: "AI that actually reduces churn requires a unified customer record — we build both"

### Retail / CPG
- Core pain: Loyalty data not feeding personalization engine; demand forecasting gaps
- Buyer language: CLV, basket size, loyalty program, omnichannel, demand sensing
- Key platforms: Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, Tableau, Snowflake
- Lead proof: loyalty platform, personalization engine, demand forecasting
- Differentiator: "Loyalty data is only valuable when it feeds the system that acts on it"

### Hi-Tech / SaaS
- Core pain: Revenue operations fragmented across CRM, billing, data — slowing GTM velocity
- Buyer language: ARR, NRR, product-led growth, RevOps, LTV/CAC, churn
- Key platforms: Sales Cloud, Revenue Cloud, CPQ, Tableau, Data Cloud
- Lead proof: RevOps unification, CPQ implementation, customer health scoring
- Differentiator: "Your Salesforce is only as good as the data feeding it — we fix both ends"

## Pitch Closing (all verticals)
- Outcome statement: "What a successful engagement looks like in your environment"
- Next step: specific — "A 2-hour architecture review with your team"
- Credential surface: Summit Partnership appears here naturally, once
