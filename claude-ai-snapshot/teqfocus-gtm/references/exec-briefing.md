# Reference: Executive Briefing Document

Use for: Pre-meeting prep docs · Board-level summaries · CIO/CDO briefing packs
Length: 1–3 pages | Format: .docx or .pdf | Audience: C-suite

## Structure

### Page 1: The Brief (always one page)

**Header:**
```
EXECUTIVE BRIEFING
[Topic / Meeting Context]
Prepared by Teqfocus | [Date]
For: [Name], [Title], [Company]
```

**The Situation (3–5 bullets):**
- What is true today — market, industry, or company-specific facts
- The shift happening now — what's changing and why it matters now
- The tension or decision the executive is facing
- Data points: verified, specific, cited. [VERIFY] if uncertain.

**The Recommendation (1–3 bullets):**
- What Teqfocus recommends and why
- Connected directly to the situation above
- One specific first step

**The Stakes (1–2 bullets):**
- Cost of acting: investment and disruption
- Cost of not acting: business risk or missed opportunity

**Prepared by:**
Name, Title, Teqfocus | contact details

### Page 2 (optional): Supporting Detail
- Expand on one key point from the brief
- Case study, data analysis, or architectural diagram reference
- Never more than 1 additional page — brevity is the point

## Tone Rules
- Concise — every sentence earns its place
- No preamble — no "Thank you for your time" openers
- No jargon without immediate context
- Recommendations must be actionable and specific
- American English throughout
